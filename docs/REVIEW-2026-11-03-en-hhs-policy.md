# Review: US compounding after the HHS policy of 30 October 2026 (English site, 3 November 2026)

**What happened.** On 30 October 2026 the US Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) published a *draft* policy
(Federal Register FR Doc. 2026-22192, 91 FR 68526; docket HHS-OASH-2026-0496, comments until 13 November 2026). Until a
final rule decides on each, HHS "does not intend to take enforcement action" against a state-licensed pharmacy, federal
facility or licensed doctor that compounds one of six peptides (BPC-157, epitalon, KPV, MOTS-c, Semax, TB-500) under
strict conditions. It is interim, not binding, and HHS may revisit it. None of the six is on the 503A list, and on
3 November 2026 the Federal Register held no proposed or final rule. The FDA has still posted no minutes of the July
2026 meeting.

**What was wrong on the live English site.** About 30 lines said compounding these six "remains prohibited until the
FDA ... completes rulemaking", or "pharmacies may not make it until the FDA makes a rule". That is now incomplete.

**What this review covers.** Every changed English line, numbered. Nothing is deployed. The Dutch pages are not
touched. UK, Canada and Australia are unchanged (this is US law; every page says it changes nothing there).

**How to answer.** Reply "approve all", or "approve all except 7, 21" (the numbers below). For the reform page
(section 3, your own signed text) please answer each line.

Sources (registered by the Dutch publishing commit, `src/data/sources/nl-recheck-2026-11.yaml`):
`fr-2026-22192-hhs-peptide-enforcement-discretion` (the notice), `hhs-2026-503a-peptide-policy` (the policy with
Appendix A and B), `federalregister-503a-search-2026-11-03` (no rule), and `fda-pcac-2026-07-meeting` (re-read

## 6. Points to know

1. **Verdicts stay "No".** In "Legal to sell or supply?" (six compound pages) and "A medicine made by a pharmacy?"
   (legal routes), compounding these six still does not meet the law; HHS only says it does not intend to act. The
   cells explain that. If you would rather show "Unknown" there, say so.
2. **What CDER recommended.** The policy's Appendix B says CDER (the FDA's drug centre) recommended against listing
   TB-500, epitalon, KPV and MOTS-c. The BPC-157 and Semax passages could not be read in full from the PDF's text, so
   the pages say nothing about CDER's view of those two. (The hhs.gov PDF answers automated requests with "403".)
3. **Forms and routes.** The source note gives Appendix B's form and route limits only for BPC-157, TB-500 and Semax.
   The KPV, epitalon and MOTS-c pages therefore give none.
4. **It is a draft.** Comments close on 13 November 2026. We should check again after that date (a final policy, a
   change, or an FDA proposed rule). The tracker item says so.
5. **Review dates.** The pages' `last_reviewed` dates are unchanged: only the US compounding parts were re-checked, and
   each changed line carries its own date.
6. **Only English.** No Dutch file is changed. No UK, Canadian or Australian statement is changed.
