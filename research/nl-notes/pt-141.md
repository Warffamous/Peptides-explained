### What was rewritten for the Netherlands

- **Status (research/nl/eu-status.md fact 7, status file):** not authorised in the EU or the Netherlands; no EMA record
  (which includes refused and withdrawn applications), not on the list of applications under evaluation (October
  2026); no product in the Geneesmiddeleninformatiebank (27 October 2026).
- **Artsenverklaring (nl-care.md facts 20–27, fact-check: confirmed):** the IGJ term "ontheffing"; the doctor's
  declaration; a Dutch pharmacy, wholesaler or manufacturer applies, never the patient; legal term up to 8 weeks; the
  IGJ can refuse and is "terughoudend" ("in uiterste gevallen", "uitdrukkelijk niet bedoeld om … te omzeilen"); for a
  medicine approved elsewhere the IGJ asks the product information of the country of origin; an ontheffing does not
  mean the insurer pays.
- **Compassionate use:** criteria (an MA application or running trials; only a manufacturer applies) and "no
  programme with bremelanotide on the CBG list" (eu-status verification log: CUP list of 10 July 2026, no
  bremelanotide). The page does not claim that no trial runs anywhere (no CTIS source is registered for the 0 hits).
- **Off-label:** only for a medicine already authorised here (Gnw art. 68).
- **Zelf invoeren (nl-law.md facts 38–43):** forbidden for a medicine without a handelsvergunning; the own-use exception
  does not cover a parcel (Rb Den Haag 2019; Customs page). Linked `/nl/legal-routes/#zelf-invoeren`.
- **"Research use only" products:** Gnw art. 40 (no supply, sale or import), and the IGJ/NVWA statement of 16 April 2026
  on disclaimers such as "voor onderzoeksdoeleinden" (written as what the IGJ and NVWA said about designer-drug
  websites, which is what the source covers). Linked `/nl/legal/research-use-only/`.
- **EU law:** no placing on the market without an MA in any member state; a member state may make a narrow exception
  for one patient on a doctor's request (Directive 2001/83 art. 5(1), as on `/nl/legal/eu/`).
- **Sport:** unchanged facts (not named on the 2026 or 2027 List; S0 does not apply because the FDA approved it; other
  classes may apply; no ruling found); the Dutch answer "Ben je sporter? Vraag het dan eerst aan de Dopingautoriteit."
  (`dopingautoriteit-geneesmiddelen-controleren`), matching the Dutch tile text.
- **Stop list (help levels of STYLE-NL §5.1 and `src/i18n/nl/warning-signs.json`):** chest pain, sudden severe
  headache/blurred vision/one-sided weakness, priapism over 3 hours and anaphylaxis → "Bel direct 112"; yellow skin or
  eyes, very dark urine → "Stop met het product. Bel direct je huisarts. Is de praktijk dicht? Bel dan de
  huisartsenpost."; new dark patches → "stop met het product en maak een afspraak met je huisarts". Thuisarts sources
  added for chest pain, stroke signs, very high blood pressure and allergy; the NHS and MedlinePlus pages stay as
  medical sources (as on `/nl/safety/warning-signs/`), not as help services.
- **Blood-pressure fold:** the NHS thresholds were replaced by Thuisarts (fetched today): high when the bovendruk is

   urgent level, as `yellow-skin-eyes` in `warning-signs.json`; faster than English, as the site's rule allows).
3. **Label use limits on an unauthorised medicine (legal).** The page keeps two label limits as risk information:
   "maar 1 dosis per 24 uur" (blood-pressure fold) and "afraadt om het vaker dan 8 keer per maand te gebruiken"

   bremelanotide has no Dutch handelsvergunning, please confirm these may stay, or should be cut to "de
   productinformatie beperkt hoe vaak je het mag gebruiken".
4. **The responder number (legal).** The KeyNumber "58 op 100 zeiden dat het hielp" (developer's responder analysis)
   stays because the science is kept identical; it sits between "+0,35" and "Vrouwen hadden niet vaker bevredigende

   Netherlands a doctor cannot prescribe it in the normal way. Keep, or say "met haar arts kan bespreken"?
6. **World context "once and short".** Because the only approval is American, the US approval appears in more than
   one sentence (Lead, Compare, one sentence after the opening, product fold, callout, US fold, harm-reduction opening,
   fact sheet "Goedgekeurd voor (VS)"), always marked and always beside the NL/EU answer. Editor: acceptable, or cut the
   US fold to one sentence (it holds the FDA's post-approval study requirements, the August 2026 warning letter and the
