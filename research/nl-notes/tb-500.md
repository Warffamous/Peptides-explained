### UNRESOLVED points and how the page handles them

- **eu-status §5.7 (national authorisations in other member states not searched):** "Of TB-500 in een ander EU-land een
  eigen, nationale goedkeuring heeft, hebben we niet nagezocht." Never "in geen enkel EU-land".
- **eu-status §5.8 (Ph. Eur. monographs not re-checked):** stated only as the FDA's finding of May 2026, verified in the
  PDF today, plus "Of het in de farmacopee van een andere lidstaat staat, weten we niet."
- **nl-law §5.1 (does "in voorraad hebben" cover a few vials at home?):** the page quotes art. 40's list, says that
  using is not in it, and says we found no law, judgment or IGJ statement on whether a few vials at home count.
- **nl-law §5.3–5.5 (what Customs does with a given parcel; intra-EU parcels):** "De Douane kan een pakket tegenhouden,
  melden bij de IGJ of in beslag nemen" (what can happen, nl-law §4.4), nothing on what usually happens.
- **nl-law §5.6 (IGJ peptide statements, media only):** not used; the Customs article is cited instead.
- **nl-law §5.2 and §5.7 (no fine for own-use import; no Dutch ruling on the research peptides):** not mentioned.
- **nl-help (NVIC does not advise the public):** poisoning is not a sign on this page; the NVIC is not named.

3. *Medical.* Stop-list levels: hives or a swollen face without throat or breathing problems → "Stop met het product en
   bel je huisarts" (Thuisarts: an appointment on a working day for swelling alone; the site keeps a slightly faster

4. *Medical.* `nhs-cancer-symptoms` has no registered Dutch equivalent (a Thuisarts or KWF page on general signs of cancer
   could replace it on Dutch pages).
5. *Editorial.* The Canadian athlete's sanction is kept as world context; drop it if the owner prefers a Dutch-only sport
   fold.
6. *Advertising test.* The developers RegeneRx and ReGenTree and the product code NL005 are named for identification, as on
