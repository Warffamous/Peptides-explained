This site is for people in the Netherlands and the rest of Europe who want to understand peptides. Some readers are only curious. Some are thinking about using a product. Some already have one. We write for all of them, without judging anyone.

We explain how each compound works, what the studies show, and what is still unknown. We explain what the law says in each country. We give practical safety information: what to check, which tests matter, and when to get help. See [start here](/en/start-here/).

<InDetail title="What we never do">

- We sell nothing, and we link to no seller.
- We give no doses, schedules or mixing sums.
- We take no money from drug makers, sellers or clinics, and we show no ads.
- We do not tell anyone to use a product, or not to use one. We give the facts so the choice is better informed.

The reasons are on our pages about the [editorial policy](/en/about/editorial-policy/) and [funding](/en/about/funding/). The law on advertising medicines is part of it. In the Netherlands, any advertising for a medicine without a licence is forbidden.<Cite id="nl-gnw-art-84-85-advertising" />

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Who checks the pages">

Drafts are prepared with help from AI tools. Every fact is then checked against the original study, regulator's page or law, and the editor oversees that work. A named doctor and a lawyer have not yet reviewed the pages. Every page shows its review status near the title. The details are in our [editorial policy](/en/about/editorial-policy/).

</InDetail>
