## 1. The scoping decision: which molecule this page grades

- **TB-500** = Ac-Leu-Lys-Lys-Thr-Glu-Thr-Gln-OH, the N-acetylated 17–23 fragment of thymosin beta-4,
  C38H68N10O14, 889.0 Da, PubChem CID 62707662, UNII QHK6Z47GTG, CAS 885340-08-9
  (`pubchem-tb-500`, `fda-pcac-2026-tb500-briefing`). Doping laboratories identified exactly this
  peptide in products called TB-500 (`esposito-2012-tb500-identity`, `ho-2012-equine-tb500`,
  `kwok-2013-horse-peptides`). FDA: "TB-500 is a common name", "thymosin beta-4 and TB-500 are not
  the same substance", and multiple salts/derivatives "including different active moieties" are sold
  under the name.
- **Thymosin beta-4** = 43 residues, N-acetylserine, INN and USAN *timbetasin*; timbetasin acetate
  = RGN-259, RGN-137, GBT-201 (`chembl-timbetasin`, `low-1981-sequence`). Recombinant 44-residue
  version NL005 (`wang-2021-rhtb4-phase1`).
- **Decision.** Sequence, identifiers and all four evidence grades describe the fragment, because
  that is what the name denotes in the FDA, WADA (S2.3 "Thymosin-β4 and its derivatives e.g.
  TB-500") and doping literature. The whole-protein trials are listed as structured studies (every
  human study that exists) with each finding prefixed by the molecule tested, and the prose explains
  the difference repeatedly. `names.full` is "Thymosin beta-4 / TB-500" as instructed;
  `names.other` deliberately does **not** list "thymosin beta-4" as a synonym.
- `category: experimental` (fragment never in formal development; the only registry record naming it,
  NCT07487363, calls itself "fictional"). The whole protein would be `investigational`.

## 6. Conflicts between sources and how they were resolved

1. **Guarnera 2010 vs register.** Paper: 73 randomised; register NCT00832091: 72 analysed. Structured
   study uses the paper (73); the healing numbers on the page come from the register and are cited to
   it.
2. **Is Philp 2003's peptide TB-500?** FDA says TB-500 "appears to have first been synthesized … in
   2003 (Philp)", but elsewhere calls Philp's peptide "non-acetylated LKKTETQ" and warns against
   extrapolation. Page: Philp = unacetylated core; "according to the FDA, TB-500 appears to have been
   made first in that work".
3. **Biçer 2026 calls TB-500 "synthetic thymosin beta-4"** and does not report the sequence. Page
   treats the molecule tested as uncertain and uses it as an example of the naming confusion.
4. **Smart 2007 vs Banerjee 2012/2013** on whether Tβ4 is needed for heart-vessel development: both
   reported; page says the natural role is disputed.
5. **Developers' review (Treadwell 2012) vs register.** "Accelerated healing by almost a month in
   those patients that did heal" vs no difference in the proportion healed. Page reports both and
   explains that the review compares only healers.
6. **WADA "since".** TB-500 was arguably covered earlier by S2's general "other growth factors"
   wording; `since: 2018-01-01` records when it was **named**. Prose says "named … in 2018".
7. **SEER-1 "Phase III" with 18 patients**, p = 0.0656 for the primary endpoint; authors describe a
   "strong efficacy trend". Page: not statistically significant; Cochrane low-certainty.
