### Human data
- PL 14736 phase 1: 32 randomised, 24 active, healthy men, enema; headache and flatulence similar to placebo; BPC-157 mostly not detected in plasma. Phase 2 UC (Ruenzi 2005, Gastroenterology 128:A584, abstract only): 53 randomised, 46 completed; DAI −3.2 vs −1.6; FDA states an "estimated difference of 1.6 points (95% CI −4.84, 1.62)". Source: `fda-pcac-2026-bpc157-briefing` §II.C and Appendix 1. The abstracts themselves are not in PubMed. Both studies are cited through the FDA summary. The structured study `ruenzi-2005-colitis-abstract` therefore points to the FDA source.
- Lee & Padgett 2021 (n=16 of 17); Lee, Walker, Ayadi 2024 (n=12); Lee & Burgess 2025 (n=2). A fourth report, added at the completion fact-check: Froum et al. 2026 (n=3 dental patients, five-drug mixture including BPC-157; `froum-2026-nerve-cases`, source registered in tb-500.yaml). PubMed records, no DOIs. Sites and compounding supply: FDA Appendix 1. Edwin Lee is lead author on all three. The 2021 and 2025 studies were at one Florida clinic and the 2024 study at another (FDA appendix). We do not name the clinics, because they offer the treatment.
- Systematic review: `poolton-2026-human-sr` (3 studies, n=30, very low certainty; searched to 1 June 2026).
- Registered trials (ClinicalTrials.gov MCP + API, 20 October 2026): NCT02637284 (status unknown, last update 12/2015, no results), NCT07437547 (phase 2 RCT, recruiting, n=120, Shenzhen, PCD Feb 2027), NCT07803250 (pilot RCT, not yet recruiting, Jan 2027), NCT07752381 (single-arm consumer gummy, completed, no results). ### Safety
- FDA nonclinical re-analysis of Xu 2020: aPTT shortened (~10%) in male rats and prolonged in dogs; ALT +24%, glucose +31% and TG +87% at recovery in low-dose female rats; TG up in female dogs; creatinine down in dogs. Histopathology data not in the manuscript. No carcinogenicity studies identified. Source: `fda-pcac-2026-bpc157-briefing` §II.D.1. Pages 35–37 of the PDF are images; I rendered and read them.
- Genotoxicity battery negative; rat embryo–fetal NOAEL; 28-day IM repeat dose: `xu-2020-preclinical-safety` (abstract) + FDA.
- PK: `he-2022-pharmacokinetics` (PMC full text). IV t½ 15.2 min (rat) and 5.27 min (dog); IM F 14–19% (rat) and 45–51% (dog); 324 + 42 rats and 6 dogs; funding NSFC + Shaanxi.

## 3. Conflicts between sources and how I resolved them

1. **Guide says six peptides; the FDA agenda shows seven.** The FDA agenda, briefing intro and questions list seven peptide groups, including emideltide (DSIP). Public reporting (McDermott Will & Emery client alert, read for verification but not cited because it is not on the allowlist) gives emideltide 6–7–1 (not recommended) and the other six recommended. The guide's six-peptide list is therefore the set *recommended*, not the set *considered*. The page says "considered seven … recommended six". **The guide should say this explicitly.**
2. **No FDA minutes.** As of 20 October 2026 the FDA meeting page (content current as of 06/08/2026) has no minutes or voting summary. The 8–6–1 count for BPC-157 rests on public reporting of the webcast (McDermott; Good Morning America; trade press), and the page says so. **The reviewer should confirm against the FDA summary minutes when posted.** Reported counts for the others, unverified: KPV 8–6–1; TB-500 8–6–1; MOTS-c 7–5–2; emideltide 6–7–1; epitalon 7–4–1 (some sources 7–5–1); Semax 8–5–1.
3. **Category 2 date.** The task asks for "FDA's 2023 placement of BPC-157 in Category 2 if verifiable". The current FDA page lists BPC-157 only as "previously in category 2" with no date. Secondary sources say 29 September 2023, but the Wayback Machine was unreachable (connection reset), and no FDA primary record with the date was retrievable. **The page therefore says "had earlier put BPC-157 in Category 2" without a date.** The reviewer could add the date from an archived FDA page.
4. **WADA status.** Józwiak et al. 2025 state BPC-157 was "temporarily banned … in 2022 (it is not currently listed …)". That is contradicted by the WADA 2026 and 2027 Lists, which name it in S0. WADA is followed, and the error is noted on the page.
5. **Angiogenesis in cell culture.** Brcic 2009 (Zagreb) reports "no direct angiogenic effect … on cell cultures". Hsieh 2017 (Taiwan) reports increased tube formation in vitro. Both are reported; neither is preferred.
6. **NO-system.** The independent lab shows eNOS-dependent effects, while the Zagreb lab reports effects that persist under L-NAME. Both are reported as unresolved.
7. **Lee 2024 design.** The paper calls itself a "pilot study" or "trial", the FDA calls it a "single arm study", and Poolton 2026 calls it "retrospective". Coded as `open-label` (prospective single-arm, per the paper's methods description in the abstract).
8. **Ruenzi 2005 CI.** The FDA reports the difference as "1.6 points (95% CI −4.84, 1.62)". The sign convention is odd, but the CI includes zero. The page says only that the CI included zero.

## 4. Editorial decisions worth reviewing

- **human_clinical = case-reports, not phase-2.** A randomised phase 2 trial exists only as a 2005 conference abstract that the FDA judged inadequate. The rubric counts "completed and published" evidence and does not say whether a conference abstract counts. I did not count it. **Contract issue.**
- **safety = uncharacterised, not partial.** There is some human exposure (about 24 in the phase 1 abstract and 30 in published reports), but there is no peer-reviewed systematic adverse-event collection, and the FDA concluded "insufficient clinical safety information to characterize the safety profile". Defensible either way; I chose the stricter grade and explained it.
- **category = experimental, not investigational**, despite one recruiting phase 2 RCT (NCT07437547). Rationale is in the YAML comment. Revisit when that trial reports.
- **Group coding.** (Superseded.) The first draft coded PLIVA (Tkalcević 2007) and the PL 14736 trial `independent`. The interrupted fact-checker recoded both `group: mixed`, and the completion fact-check confirmed the basis in PubMed: Mildner B (Sikirić 1993) and Perović D (Sikirić 1997, Staresinic 2003) are co-authors of Tkalcević 2007, and the group's own abstracts describe PLIVA's clinical development of their compound. Ruenzi 2005 itself lists no Zagreb author; it is coded `mixed` as part of the same PL 14736 programme (Veljaca 2002/2003 abstracts list Mildner B). Both keep `funding: industry`. The vocabulary has no value for "developer of the originator's compound".
- **Human doses deliberately omitted.** The FDA and the Lee papers give human amounts (mg intra-articular, intravesical, IV; mg enema). None appear on the page, to avoid any reading as guidance. Animal doses appear once, in `<Q>`, framed as study design (Staresinic 2003, to show the three doses tested a million-fold apart; since the completion fact-check the page no longer claims all three worked equally, because the abstract does not report results by dose). He 2022 states a "proposed clinical dose" with human-equivalent conversions; it is deliberately not reported.
- **Names not used**: the compounding pharmacies, clinics and "research" vendors named in FDA documents, and the brand of the consumer gummy in NCT07752381. The sponsor of NCT07437547 (Hudson Biotech) is not named either; only the site country is given.
- **Becaplermin (Regranex)** is named as an authorised medicine for identification only, as the guide allows.
- **The withdrawn 2025 Curr Neuropharmacol paper** (PMID 39865815, "Withdrawn: …", Zagreb group) is not mentioned. The reason for withdrawal is unknown, and mentioning it could imply misconduct unfairly.
