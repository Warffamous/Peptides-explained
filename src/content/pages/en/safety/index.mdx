<Callout type="stop" title="Feeling unwell after an injection?">

Go to the [warning signs](/en/safety/warning-signs/) now. They tell you when to call your local emergency number and when to see a doctor today. They also tell you when to stop and call your GP.

</Callout>

<Lead question="Why give safety advice if you do not encourage use?">Because clean steps prevent many infections. They cannot fix what is inside the vial, though.</Lead>

<Diagram id="what-clean-steps-can-fix" caption="Clean steps prevent many infections. They cannot fix a bad product.">

| Clean steps help prevent | Clean steps cannot fix |
|---|---|
| Abscesses | A product of unknown quality |
| Blood infections | Germs that were already inside the vial before it was opened |
| The spread of HIV and hepatitis | |

</Diagram>

<Cite id="who-2010-injection-toolkit,drugsinfo-injecteren,fda-unapproved-glp1-concerns,smith-2013-fungal-methylprednisolone" />

These pages are for people who use peptides, or are about to. We do not encourage anyone to use them. But if you are going to inject, we want you to do it with as little harm as possible.

The route with the fewest unknowns is a prescribed medicine from a pharmacy, with a doctor who follows you. A product sold online as a "research chemical" is of unknown quality. Even perfect technique cannot fix what is inside the vial.<Cite id="fda-unapproved-glp1-concerns,smith-2013-fungal-methylprednisolone" /> See [talking to your doctor](/en/talking-to-your-doctor/).

These pages give no doses, no mixing sums and no sources of supply.

<InDetail title="How Dutch harm-reduction services work">

This is how Dutch harm-reduction services work with people who inject drugs. They give practical advice about new needles, not sharing equipment and safe disposal.<Cite id="drugsinfo-injecteren,mainline-steroids-safe-injecting" /> Simple steps like these help prevent abscesses, blood infections and the spread of HIV and hepatitis.<Cite id="who-2010-injection-toolkit,drugsinfo-injecteren" /> We apply the same approach to peptides.

</InDetail>
