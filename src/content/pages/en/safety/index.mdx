<Callout type="stop" title="Feeling unwell after an injection?">

Go to the [warning signs](/en/safety/warning-signs/) now. They tell you when to call your local emergency number and when to get medical help today. They also tell you when to stop and contact your doctor.

</Callout>

<Lead question="Why does this site give safety advice?">Because clean steps prevent many infections. They cannot fix what is inside the vial, though.</Lead>

<Diagram id="what-clean-steps-can-fix" caption="Clean steps prevent many infections. They cannot fix a bad product.">

| Clean steps help prevent | Clean steps cannot fix |
|---|---|
| Abscesses | A product of unknown quality |
| Blood infections | Germs that were already inside the vial before it was opened |
| The spread of HIV and hepatitis | |

</Diagram>

<Cite id="who-2010-injection-toolkit,fda-unapproved-glp1-concerns,smith-2013-fungal-methylprednisolone" />

These pages are for people who use peptides, or are about to. We do not encourage use, and we do not judge it. If you inject, we want it to cause as little harm as possible. If you already have a product, start with the [checks before you use it](/en/safety/product-risk/#if-you-already-have-a-product).

The route with the fewest unknowns is a prescribed medicine from a pharmacy, with a doctor who follows you. A product sold online as a "research chemical" is of unknown quality. Even perfect technique cannot fix what is inside the vial.<Cite id="fda-unapproved-glp1-concerns,smith-2013-fungal-methylprednisolone" /> See [talking to your doctor](/en/talking-to-your-doctor/).

These pages give no doses, no mixing sums and no sources of supply.

Harm-reduction services can help too. A needle and syringe programme gives out new injecting equipment, usually free, and takes back used needles. Some say plainly that they welcome people who inject performance and image enhancing drugs.<Cite id="uk-ni-pha-harm-reduction-booklet,ie-hse-anabolic-steroids-reducing-harm,au-healthdirect-needlestick" /> Local drug services can also test for HIV and hepatitis.<Cite id="uk-nhs-drug-addiction-help" /> To find one, ask your doctor, a pharmacist or your local health service.

<InDetail title="Finding a harm-reduction service in your country">

- **United States.** Needle and syringe programmes are called syringe services programs. We did not find a current national list of them on an official US site when we checked in October 2026. Ask your doctor, a pharmacist or your local health department.
- **United Kingdom.** In England, a GP or your local drug service can help, and [FRANK](https://talktofrank.com/contact-frank) can point you to support near you. In Wales, [DAN 24/7](https://dan247.org.uk/) is the drugs and alcohol helpline. In Scotland, used equipment goes back to an injecting equipment provider. In Northern Ireland, needle and syringe exchanges are free, confidential and anonymous.<Cite id="uk-nhs-drug-addiction-help,uk-frank-contact,uk-wales-dan-247,uk-scot-nhsinform-injecting-advice,uk-ni-pha-harm-reduction-booklet" />
- **Canada.** Health Canada's page [Get help with substance use](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/substance-use/get-help-with-substance-use.html) lists services for each province and territory.<Cite id="ca-hc-get-help-substance-use" />
- **Australia.** Every state and territory has a needle and syringe program. The [National Alcohol and Other Drug Hotline](https://www.health.gov.au/contacts/national-alcohol-and-other-drug-hotline) puts you through to the service for your state or territory.<Cite id="au-healthdirect-needlestick,au-health-aod-hotline" />
- **Ireland and New Zealand.** In Ireland, HSE needle exchange services are free and confidential. In New Zealand, Health New Zealand's page on [safe sharps disposal](https://www.healthnz.govt.nz/health-topics/tests-and-treatments/medicines/safe-sharps-disposal) links to the needle exchange programme.<Cite id="ie-hse-anabolic-steroids-reducing-harm,nz-healthnz-safe-sharps-disposal" />

**Drug checking.** Some public health services test a sample of a substance to see what is in it. One example is [WEDINOS](https://wedinos.wales/about-us/), run with Public Health Wales for people in Wales. It was set up to test unknown drugs and new psychoactive substances, so ask any service first whether it can test a peptide.<Cite id="uk-wales-wedinos-about" /> We did not check drug-checking services in the other countries. A result covers only the sample that was tested. See [what a lab report can and cannot tell you](/en/safety/product-risk/).

</InDetail>
