## What counts as a correction

<Lead question="What do you count as a correction?">Any change to a fact, number, source, grade or legal status. Small typos are logged too.</Lead>

<Diagram id="correction-kinds" caption="The five kinds of entry in our corrections log.">

| Kind of entry | What it means | Does the page itself show it? |
|---|---|---|
| Publication | A version of the site, or a new page, went online | No; this is where a page's record starts |
| Substantive correction | A fact, number, source, evidence grade or safety statement was wrong, and we changed it | Yes, with the date |
| Clarification | The facts were right, but the words could be misread, so we reworded them | Yes, with the date |
| Regulatory update | A law or a regulator's decision changed after we checked it, and we brought the page up to date | Yes, with the date |
| Minor correction | A typo, a broken link or a layout fault | Yes, with the date; small fixes may be logged together |

</Diagram>

We do not change a page quietly when the change matters. A substantive entry says what was wrong, what the page says now, and which source shows it. Each corrected page also shows the date near its title, with a link to this log.

## How fast we act

<Lead question="How fast do you fix mistakes?">Safety mistakes first, as soon as we have checked them. Other mistakes within days.</Lead>

<Diagram id="correction-speed" caption="What we do first, and how fast we aim to act, once a mistake is confirmed.">

| Kind of mistake | Example | What we do | We aim to act within |
|---|---|---|---|
| Could affect someone's safety | A wrong warning sign or a wrong step in an emergency | Correct the passage, or take it down until it is right; then look for the same mistake on other pages | One working day |
| Substantive | A wrong number, source, status or evidence grade | Correct and log it | Seven days |
| A law or status changed | A regulator decides something new | Update the page and its "checked on" date | As soon as we learn of it |
| Minor | A typo or a broken link | Fix it; log it with other small fixes | The next update |

</Diagram>

These times start when we have confirmed the mistake against the original source. Checking can take longer when the source is hard to get.
