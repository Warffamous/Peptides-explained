This site is for readers in the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada and Australia, with notes for Ireland and New Zealand. Some readers are only curious. Some are thinking about using a product. Some already have one. We write for all of them, without judging anyone.

We explain how each compound works, what the studies show, what is still unknown, and what the law says in each country. We give practical safety information. See [start here](/en/start-here/).

<InDetail title="What we never do, and why">

- We sell nothing, and we link to no seller.
- We give no doses, schedules or mixing sums.
- We take no money from drug makers, sellers or clinics, and we show no ads.
- We do not tell anyone to use a product, or not to use one. We give the facts so the choice is better informed.

{/* residue-ok: publisher */}
This site is published from the Netherlands, so Dutch law decides what it may say. That law forbids any advertising for a medicine without a licence.<Cite id="nl-gnw-art-84-85-advertising" /> That is why we sell nothing, link to no seller and give no doses. Our readers' countries have similar rules: in the UK, Canada and Australia, the law also forbids advertising a medicine that is not licensed there.<Cite id="uk-hmr-2012-reg-279,ca-food-and-drug-regulations,au-tg-act-advertising,au-tg-regs-reg-7-advertising-prescribed-goods" /> More is on our pages about the [editorial policy](/en/about/editorial-policy/) and [funding](/en/about/funding/).

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Who checks the pages">

Drafts are prepared with help from AI tools. Every fact is then checked against the original study, regulator's page or law, and the editor oversees that work. A named doctor and a lawyer have not yet reviewed the pages. Every page shows its review status near the title. The details are in our [editorial policy](/en/about/editorial-policy/).

</InDetail>
