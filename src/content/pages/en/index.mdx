## The most important distinction

<Lead question="Does the same name mean the same product?">No. The same name can mean a checked medicine or an unchecked vial.</Lead>

<Compare title="A pharmacy medicine and an online vial can share a name. They are not the same thing." detail="Semaglutide as an example. What regulators say about each.">

| | Semaglutide from a pharmacy | "Research semaglutide" sold online |
|---|---|---|
| An authorised medicine? | <Verdict is="yes" /> Authorised in the EU; only on prescription<Cite id="ema-epar-ozempic,ema-epar-rybelsus,ema-epar-wegovy" /> | <Verdict is="no" /> It is not that medicine. The UK regulator says GLP-1 powder in vials is not authorised<Cite id="mhra-glp1-what-you-need-to-know" /> |
| Benefits and risks weighed by a regulator? | <Verdict is="yes" /> Before it was authorised<Cite id="ema-epar-wegovy,cbg-glp1-position" /> | <Verdict is="no" /> The US regulator says it does not check these copies for harm, effect or quality<Cite id="fda-unapproved-glp1-concerns" /> |
| Is what is inside checked? | <Verdict is="yes" /> The EU regulator checked how well it is made before it was allowed<Cite id="ema-epar-wegovy" /> | <Verdict is="no" /> No regulator has checked what is in it. The Dutch regulator says it is unclear what is inside<Cite id="cbg-glp1-position,fda-unapproved-glp1-concerns" /> |

</Compare>

The same name can mean two very different things. **Semaglutide** from a pharmacy is an authorised medicine: a regulator has checked its quality, its effects and its risks.<Cite id="ema-epar-wegovy,cbg-glp1-position" /> A vial sold online as "research semaglutide" is not that medicine. No regulator has checked what is in it.<Cite id="cbg-glp1-position,fda-unapproved-glp1-concerns" />

Most of the other peptides people talk about, such as [BPC-157](/en/compounds/bpc-157/), have no authorisation anywhere.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2026-bpc157-briefing,fda-pcac-2026-tb500-briefing,ema-medicines-search" /> Their evidence comes mostly from animals.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2026-bpc157-briefing" /> This site shows the shape of that evidence for every compound, in the same way, so you can compare honestly.
