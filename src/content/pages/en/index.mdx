## The most important distinction

The same name can mean two very different things. **Semaglutide** from a pharmacy is an authorised medicine: a regulator has checked its quality, its effects and its risks. A vial sold online as "research semaglutide" is not that medicine. Nobody has checked what is in it.

Most of the other peptides people talk about, such as [BPC-157](/en/compounds/bpc-157/), have no authorisation anywhere. Their evidence comes mostly from animals. This site shows the shape of that evidence for every compound, in the same way, so you can compare honestly.

## Who this is for

- People who have already decided to use a peptide, and want to be as safe as possible.
- People who are curious and want to know what they don't know.
- Doctors, physiotherapists and trainers who keep getting asked.
- Partners and parents who are worried.
