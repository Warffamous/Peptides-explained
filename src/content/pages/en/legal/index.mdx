Every EU country follows the same basic rules. A product that is offered as a treatment, or that can change how the body works, counts as a medicine. A medicine needs a licence before anyone may sell it.<Cite id="eu-directive-2001-83" /> Advertising a medicine without a licence is forbidden.<Cite id="eu-directive-2001-83" /> Penalties and enforcement differ by country.

We describe the law from the laws themselves and from official guidance, with the date we checked. Laws change, so each page says when we last checked it. We follow coming changes on [what's changing](/en/legal/whats-changing/).

This section does not tell anyone how to get round the law, where to find a product, or how to use one. For safety information, see [safety](/en/safety/).
