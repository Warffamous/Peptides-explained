<Lead question="What is Selank?">A lab-made chain of seven amino acids. It is a medicine in Russia.</Lead>

<Compare title="Selank is a medicine in Russia. The US, the UK, Canada and Australia have not approved it." detail="What is known about the substance sold under this name, checked on 22 and 23 October 2026.">

Online it is sold as powder for injection or as nose sprays.<Cite id="vanhee-2020-nootropic-peptides" /> A Russian registration says nothing about products sold elsewhere under the name.

<InDetail title="The molecule, and what a Russian registration means">

Selank is <Seq>Thr-Lys-Pro-Arg-Pro-Gly-Pro</Seq>, formula C33H57N11O9, mass <Q>751.9 g/mol</Q>.<Cite id="pubchem-selank" /> Its first four amino acids are tuftsin, a small peptide cut from antibodies that stirs up germ-eating immune cells.<Cite id="fridkin-1989-tuftsin" /> The extra Pro-Gly-Pro is the same ending as in Semax.<Cite id="pubchem-semax" /> It was developed in Russia and is also called TP-7.<Cite id="deigin-2022-russian-peptides,fda-category-2-safety-risks" />

A registration in Russia lets the product be sold there. It is not an assessment by a regulator in the US, the UK, Canada or Australia, and it does not make Selank legal to supply as a medicine in any of them.<Cite id="usc-21-355,uk-hmr-2012-reg-46,ca-food-and-drug-regulations,au-tg-act-s19b-19d-offences" />

</InDetail>

</Section>

<Lead question="How does it work?">Not well understood. In lab tests it slows the breakdown of natural calming peptides.</Lead>

<Section id="evidence">

<Lead question="Does it work?">Not known. Small Russian studies say anxiety fell, but no patient study used a dummy drug.</Lead>

<KeyNumber title="Even the biggest study had no dummy drug" value="70" label="people with anxiety: 40 got Selank plus another drug, 30 got that drug alone" who="Medvedev 2015: Russian-language paper; no placebo group; allocation not described" source="medvedev-2015-selank-add-on" level="human-limited" />

Without a placebo group, a study cannot separate a drug's effect from the natural ups and downs of anxiety. All three patient studies include a developer among the authors.<Cite id="zozulia-2008-selank-gad,medvedev-2014-selank-phenazepam,medvedev-2015-selank-add-on" />

<InDetail title="Every study in one table">

<StudyTable />

About 50 cell and animal studies exist. Nearly all come from a few Moscow institutes linked to the developers.<Cite id="pubmed-semax-selank-count-2026-10-22" /> The only study from outside Russia we found is a laboratory analysis of seized products.<Cite id="vanhee-2020-nootropic-peptides" />

</InDetail>

In sport its status is unclear everywhere. It is not named on the list, and because it is registered in Russia, the catch-all ban (S0) may not apply. Ask your anti-doping organisation.<Cite id="wada-2026-list-en,wada-2027-list-en,wada-2027-list-explanatory-note,deigin-2022-russian-peptides" />

<InDetail title="Status in each country, and in sport">

<RegulatoryTable />

The FDA lists Selank acetate (TP-7) among substances once in category 2, for possible significant safety risks. It names a risk of immune reactions and says it lacks important safety information.<Cite id="fda-category-2-safety-risks" /> Selank was not among the seven peptides the FDA's advisers discussed in July 2026.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2026-07-meeting" />

In sport, S0 bans substances that no government health authority has approved. Selank is registered in Russia, so S0 may not cover it. WADA warns that peptides that are not named may still be banned under another section.<Cite id="wada-2026-list-en,wada-2027-list-explanatory-note" /> Only an anti-doping organisation can give a ruling.

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="The UK, Canada and Australia">

- **United Kingdom.** The MHRA's database of licensed medicines has no Selank product.<Cite id="mhra-products-search-status-d-2026-10-23" /> It is not a controlled drug under the Misuse of Drugs Act.<Cite id="uk-mda-1971-sch-2" />
- **Canada.** No Selank product has a Drug Identification Number (DIN), the number every authorised drug carries.<Cite id="ca-dpd-search-canada-legal-2026-10-23,ca-dpd-extract-search-merge-2026-10-24" /> Health Canada says injectable peptides are generally regulated as prescription drugs. In 2025 it named Selank among unauthorised injectable peptides seized from a clinic and from an online seller.<Cite id="ca-hc-2026-04-think-twice-peptides,ca-hc-recalls-search-status-d-2026-10-23" />
- **Australia.** Selank is not in the Australian Register of Therapeutic Goods (ARTG), so it is not approved. The Poisons Standard does not name it, so it has no schedule.<Cite id="au-artg-search-status-d-2026-10-23,au-poisons-standard-2026-10" /> A product that is not in the ARTG still may not be imported or supplied unless an exemption applies.<Cite id="au-tg-act-s19b-19d-offences,au-tga-2026-04-peptide-responsibilities" /> Since June 2026 the regulator, the TGA, has made unapproved peptides a priority for its checks.<Cite id="au-tga-2026-06-peptide-compliance-priority" />

More: the law in [the US](/en/legal/us/), [the UK](/en/legal/uk/), [Canada](/en/legal/ca/) and [Australia](/en/legal/au/).

<Section id="risks">

<Lead question="What are the risks?">Barely tested in people. Online products are not checked.</Lead>

<Compare title="Several harms are possible. None has been studied properly in people." detail="What the FDA and the lab studies suggest, as of 22 October 2026.">

| Possible harm | Shown in people? |
|---|---|
| An immune reaction to the peptide or its impurities | <Verdict is="unknown" /> The FDA names this risk for some routes.<Cite id="fda-category-2-safety-risks" /> |
| Slower blood clotting, which could matter with blood thinners | <Verdict is="unknown" /> Seen in a lab clotting test only.<Cite id="rogozinskaya-2017-selank-anticoagulant" /> |
| A product that is not what the label says | <Verdict is="unknown" /> Two unidentified seized products turned out to hold Selank.<Cite id="vanhee-2020-nootropic-peptides" /> |
| Harm from long-term use | <Verdict is="unknown" /> The FDA lacks the information to judge it.<Cite id="fda-category-2-safety-risks" /> |

</Compare>

<InDetail title="What is known, and what is not">

The Russian patient studies were short and compared Selank with benzodiazepines, or added it to one, so they cannot show rare or delayed harms.<Cite id="zozulia-2008-selank-gad,medvedev-2014-selank-phenazepam,medvedev-2015-selank-add-on" /> Products sold online are made outside any checked supply chain. Investigators had to test two unknown seized products to find out that they held Selank and Semax.<Cite id="vanhee-2020-nootropic-peptides" /> See [product risk](/en/safety/product-risk/).

</InDetail>

<ProductCheck />

### When to stop and get help

<Callout type="stop" title="Stop and get help if" id="stop-now">
- you get hives, swelling of the face or throat, or difficulty breathing (an allergic reaction);
- an injection site becomes hot, red, swollen or painful (infection or contamination);
- you get a fever or chills, or feel very ill, after an injection.

If it is severe, call your local emergency number.
</Callout>

<Cite id="nhs-anaphylaxis,nhs-cellulitis,nhs-sepsis" />

<WarningSigns only="general,injection,allergic" />

<InDetail title="Interactions, checks, and who should not use it">

A doctor can help you weigh these (see [talking to your doctor](/en/talking-to-your-doctor/)):

- **Blood thinners**, because Selank slowed clotting in a lab test. This has not been tested in people.<Cite id="rogozinskaya-2017-selank-anticoagulant" />
- **The anxiety itself.** A doctor can help find out what is behind it and talk through treatment options.
- **Anti-doping rules**, if you compete: ask your anti-doping organisation first.

There are no data in pregnancy or breastfeeding. See [sterile technique](/en/safety/sterile-technique/).

</InDetail>
