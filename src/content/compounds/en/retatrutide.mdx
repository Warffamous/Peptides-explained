<Cite id="piatkowski-2026-retatrutide-product-testing,rubin-2026-jama-news,nl-geneesmiddelenwet" />

It is investigational. As of 20 October 2026, no regulator anywhere has authorised it, and it is not on the European Medicines Agency (EMA) list of applications under assessment dated 5 October 2026.<Cite id="ema-medicines-json,ema-2026-10-under-evaluation,fda-unapproved-glp1-concerns,mhra-2026-10-counterfeit-pens" /> In a filing with the US financial regulator in August 2026, Lilly said that it plans to ask the US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) for approval in the first quarter of 2027.<Cite id="sec-lilly-2026-q2-results" />

<InDetail title="The developer, and why it is legal only inside a trial">

Retatrutide is an injectable peptide made by Eli Lilly, also known by its development code LY3437943. It switches on three hormone receptors at once: the receptors for GIP, GLP-1 and glucagon.<Cite id="chembl-retatrutide,coskun-2022-ly3437943-discovery" /> That is why it is called a triple agonist.

<Callout type="legal" title="Investigational means: in practice, legal only inside a trial">
A medicine needs a marketing authorisation before anyone may sell or supply it. Dutch law forbids selling, supplying, stocking or importing a medicine without one. The exceptions are narrow: medicines for research, formal compassionate-use programmes, and a special exemption from the health inspectorate.<Cite id="nl-geneesmiddelenwet" /> So outside an approved trial, every product sold as "retatrutide" is unauthorised, whatever its label says. See [research use only](/en/legal/research-use-only/).
</Callout>

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="The molecule, and where it came from">

### What the molecule is

Retatrutide is a chain of 39 amino acids that starts <Seq>Tyr-Aib-Gln-Gly-Thr-Phe</Seq>.<Cite id="gsrs-retatrutide" /> Four features stand out:<Cite id="gsrs-retatrutide,li-2024-retatrutide-structure" />

- **Positions 2 and 20:** Aib (2-aminoisobutyric acid), an amino acid that the body does not use in proteins.
- **Position 13:** α-methyl-leucine, another non-coded amino acid.
- **Position 17:** a lysine that carries a short spacer, a glutamic-acid linker and a 20-carbon fatty diacid.
- **The end of the chain** is an amide rather than an acid.

Its formula is C221H342N46O68 and its mass is about <Q>4731 Da</Q>.<Cite id="pubchem-retatrutide" /> Tirzepatide carries the same kind of fatty-acid tail; its EU label says the tail binds albumin in the blood and lengthens its half-life.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" /> <Claim level="hypothesis">Retatrutide's tail probably works the same way</Claim>; its measured half-life in people is about six days.<Cite id="urva-2022-ly3437943-phase-1b" />

### Where it came from

Lilly described it in 2022. <Claim level="in-vitro">In cell assays it was about equally active at the glucagon and GLP-1 receptors and more active at the GIP receptor</Claim>: about 0.3, 0.4 and 8.9 times as active as the body's own hormones.<Cite id="coskun-2022-ly3437943-discovery,sanyal-2024-retatrutide-masld" /> The first study in people started in March 2019.<Cite id="ctgov-nct03841630" /> [Semaglutide](/en/compounds/semaglutide/) acts on one of these receptors (GLP-1), and [tirzepatide](/en/compounds/tirzepatide/), also made by Lilly, acts on two (GIP and GLP-1). Retatrutide adds glucagon.<Cite id="ema-epar-wegovy,ema-epar-mounjaro,chembl-retatrutide" />

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Why so many people have heard of it">

Its trial results drew wide attention (see the evidence below), and long before any approval it reached a grey market. The UK regulator dismantled an illegal factory making retatrutide and tirzepatide pens in October 2025, and seized more than 12,000 fake pens in October 2026.<Cite id="mhra-2025-retatrutide-seizure,mhra-2026-10-counterfeit-pens" /> In July 2026 it warned people not to be tempted by "the online hype around retatrutide".<Cite id="mhra-2026-07-no-summer-shortcut" /> In the United States, the FDA has warned companies that sold it falsely labelled "for research purposes".<Cite id="fda-unapproved-glp1-concerns" />

The trial evidence on this page belongs to a product of known content, given in a supervised trial. A vial or pen bought online shares the name and none of those guarantees. The comparison at the top of this section sets out the differences row by row.

</InDetail>

<Section id="mechanism">

<Lead question="What does it do in the body?">It acts like three hormones. People feel less hungry. In mice, the body also burned more energy.</Lead>

Retatrutide switches on three receptors and keeps doing so for days. The GLP-1 and GIP receptors are the ones tirzepatide uses. The glucagon receptor is the new part, and it raises most of the open questions.

<Diagram id="retatrutide-three-receptors" caption="One molecule, three receptors. What each does, and how well it is shown in people.">

| Receptor | Where it is mainly found | What switching it on does | How well this is shown for retatrutide |
|---|---|---|---|
| GLP-1 receptor | Pancreas, brain, gut, heart | More insulin when glucose is high, less hunger, slower stomach emptying | Less hunger and lower HbA1c shown in people |
| GIP receptor | Pancreas, brain, fat tissue | More insulin when glucose is high; effects on fat tissue and appetite | Fat and brain effects mainly in animals |
| Glucagon receptor | Liver above all | Liver releases sugar and burns fat and amino acids; more energy used | Extra energy use shown in mice; less liver fat in people, partly through weight loss |
| All three together | | In an indirect comparison of separate trials, somewhat larger average weight loss than with single or dual agonists | Weight loss shown in people; the share due to glucagon is not known |

</Diagram>

<Mechanism />

### What each step rests on

**Receptors and half-life.** <Claim level="in-vitro">Cryo-electron microscopy shows retatrutide bound to each receptor, each coupled to the Gs protein that raises cAMP</Claim>.<Cite id="li-2024-retatrutide-structure" /> The activity ratios come from cell lines.<Cite id="sanyal-2024-retatrutide-masld" /> The half-life of about six days was measured in 72 people with type 2 diabetes.<Cite id="urva-2022-ly3437943-phase-1b" /> So about half of a dose is still in the body six days after an injection, and the drug leaves the body slowly when injections stop. In four people who injected it twice, a week apart, it was still found in the blood 56 days after the first injection.<Cite id="carneiro-2026-retatrutide-antidoping" />

**Appetite and insulin.** <Claim level="human-controlled">In an analysis of a phase 2 trial in 275 adults with type 2 diabetes, people on higher doses reported less hunger and less urge to overeat than on placebo</Claim>.<Cite id="kanu-2025-retatrutide-appetite" /> HbA1c fell in every published diabetes trial.<Cite id="rosenstock-2023-retatrutide-t2d-phase-2,bajaj-2026-transcend-t2d-1,bellido-2026-triumph-2" />

**The glucagon part.** Glucagon normally raises blood sugar. The idea is that the GLP-1 and GIP parts hold glucose down, while the glucagon part acts on the liver and on energy use.<Cite id="elmendorf-2026-glucagon-iuphar-review" /> <Claim level="animal">In obese mice, the glucagon part increased energy expenditure on top of eating less</Claim>.<Cite id="coskun-2022-ly3437943-discovery" /> <Claim level="human-limited">In 11 healthy men, a glucagon infusion raised energy use by 15%</Claim>, but that was glucagon itself, given briefly.<Cite id="salem-2016-glucagon-energy-expenditure" /> A Lilly study that measured energy use in 85 people with obesity finished in August 2025; no results were public by 20 October 2026.<Cite id="ctgov-nct06313528" />

**The liver.** <Claim level="in-vitro">In human liver cells, retatrutide lowered the release of ANGPTL3/8, a protein that slows the breakdown of blood fats, and a glucagon-receptor blocker stopped this</Claim>.<Cite id="wen-2025-retatrutide-angptl38" /> In the liver-fat substudy, liver fat fell most in people who lost the most weight, and ketones, a sign of fat burning, rose by about 80% to 180% at the three higher dose levels, without ketoacidosis.<Cite id="sanyal-2024-retatrutide-masld" /> Weight loss and a direct liver effect probably both contribute; the trials cannot separate them.

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="The open question: what does the glucagon part add?">

<Callout type="uncertain" title="What does the glucagon part add in people?">
In an indirect comparison of separate trials, average weight loss beyond placebo was 22.1% with retatrutide and 19.3% with tirzepatide, and the uncertainty ranges of the two overlapped.<Cite id="chen-2026-glp1-network-meta-analysis" /> <Claim level="hypothesis">Part of the extra loss may come from more energy being used, as in mice</Claim>. But no published human study separates the two, and in one independent study obese mice lost 31% of their weight with no significant change in energy expenditure.<Cite id="briand-2026-retatrutide-mash-models" /> A head-to-head trial against tirzepatide in 800 adults is due to finish its main phase in November 2026.<Cite id="ctgov-nct06662383" />
</Callout>

The glucagon part may also have costs: in mice it speeds the heart's pacemaker, and glucagon breaks down amino acids, which could cost lean tissue. Both are discussed under the risks.<Cite id="neumann-2025-retatrutide-mouse-atria,elmendorf-2026-glucagon-iuphar-review" />

</InDetail>

<Section id="evidence">

<InDetail title="What the trials found, and what is not yet published">

### Early trials

In a 12-week phase 1b trial in 72 adults with type 2 diabetes, <Claim level="human-controlled">glucose and HbA1c fell at the three highest dose levels, and weight fell by up to about 9 kg more than with placebo</Claim>; 29 people left early.<Cite id="urva-2022-ly3437943-phase-1b" /> In a 48-week phase 2 trial in 338 adults with obesity or overweight, <Claim level="human-controlled">mean weight fell by up to 24.2% at the highest dose, against 2.1% with placebo</Claim>.<Cite id="jastreboff-2023-retatrutide-phase-2" /> In a phase 2 trial in 281 adults with type 2 diabetes, <Claim level="human-controlled">HbA1c fell by up to 2.02 percentage points at 24 weeks, against 0.01 with placebo and 1.41 with dulaglutide, an authorised GLP-1 medicine</Claim>.<Cite id="rosenstock-2023-retatrutide-t2d-phase-2" />

### Phase 3: what has been published

TRANSCEND-T2D-1 randomised 537 adults with type 2 diabetes on diet and exercise only, in the USA, Mexico and India. <Claim level="human-controlled">Over 40 weeks HbA1c fell by 1.69 to 1.94 percentage points across three dose levels, against 0.81 with placebo; weight fell by 11.5% to 15.3%, against 2.6%</Claim>.<Cite id="bajaj-2026-transcend-t2d-1" />

TRIUMPH-2 randomised 1,152 adults with obesity and type 2 diabetes in eight countries. <Claim level="human-controlled">Over 80 weeks weight fell by 11.9%, 16.8% and 18.8% at the low, middle and high dose levels, against 5.1% with placebo</Claim>.<Cite id="bellido-2026-triumph-2" />

TRIUMPH-1 randomised 2,339 adults with obesity but no diabetes. <Claim level="human-controlled">Over 80 weeks mean weight fell by 17.6%, 23.7% and 25.0%, against 3.9% with placebo</Claim>. Knee pain improved more than with placebo in 574 people with knee osteoarthritis, and breathing pauses and shallow breaths during sleep fell by 22.8 to 34.3 an hour, against 9.6 with placebo, in 243 people with sleep apnoea.<Cite id="jastreboff-2026-triumph-1" /> We could read only the abstracts of TRIUMPH-1 and TRIUMPH-2.

### Announced by the company, not yet published

TRIUMPH-3 and TRIUMPH-4 have finished, and Lilly has announced positive results.<Cite id="ctgov-nct05882045,ctgov-nct05931367,sec-lilly-2025-q4-results,sec-lilly-2026-q2-results,kaczynski-2026-retatrutide-review" /> Company announcements give headline (topline) numbers before independent experts have checked the full data. <InDetail title="Substudies, and research outside the company">

### What the substudies add

- **Liver fat.** In 98 adults with at least 10% liver fat, <Claim level="human-controlled">liver fat fell by up to 82% at 24 weeks</Claim>, without signs of liver toxicity. The substudy used MRI rather than biopsy, made many comparisons without correction, and lacked 48-week scans for 56% of people.<Cite id="sanyal-2024-retatrutide-masld" /> A large liver-outcome trial is recruiting, including in the Netherlands.<Cite id="ctgov-nct07165028" />
- **Body composition.** In the diabetes trial, body fat fell by about a quarter at higher doses; only 103 of 189 people had scans at both start and week 36. The company authors judged the share of lean tissue lost to be similar to other obesity drugs.<Cite id="coskun-2025-retatrutide-body-composition" />
- **Kidneys and blood fats.** Post hoc analyses of the two phase 2 trials, written by or with Lilly scientists, found less protein (albumin) in the urine and lower triglycerides and LDL cholesterol.<Cite id="heerspink-2025-retatrutide-kidney,wen-2025-retatrutide-angptl38" />

### Independent research

No group outside Lilly has run a trial in people. About 16 small animal studies come from independent groups.<Cite id="pubmed-retatrutide-count-2026-10-20" /> In one, <Claim level="animal">retatrutide lowered weight and liver fat in obese mice and hamsters</Claim>; in another, it lowered weight in mice lacking a key appetite receptor. In both, lean mass fell along with fat in mice.<Cite id="briand-2026-retatrutide-mash-models,hitaka-2026-retatrutide-mc4r" /> Independent studies of heart tissue, products and use outside trials are covered under the risks.

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="How it compares, and what the wait costs">

### How it compares with other medicines

An independent network meta-analysis of 58 trials (24,214 adults without diabetes) estimated <Claim level="human-controlled">22.1% more weight loss than placebo with retatrutide, and 19.3% with tirzepatide</Claim>, with low-certainty evidence that more people stop retatrutide.<Cite id="chen-2026-glp1-network-meta-analysis" /> These are indirect comparisons; direct trials against semaglutide and tirzepatide have not reported.<Cite id="ctgov-nct06260722,ctgov-nct06662383" />

### Where the evidence is genuinely promising, and what the wait costs

This is not a weak evidence base. Three placebo-controlled phase 3 trials in more than 4,000 people, published in peer-reviewed journals, show large and consistent falls in weight, lower HbA1c in people with diabetes, and benefits on knee pain and sleep apnoea.<Cite id="bajaj-2026-transcend-t2d-1,bellido-2026-triumph-2,jastreboff-2026-triumph-1" /> For people with severe obesity, that is a real prospect.

The wait has costs. Most people who see the headlines cannot get the drug legally outside a trial, and a grey market of unknown products has filled the gap.<Cite id="mhra-2026-10-counterfeit-pens,piatkowski-2026-retatrutide-product-testing" /> The wait also has reasons: questions about heart rate, skin sensations, lean mass and long-term outcomes are still open.<Cite id="ctgov-nct06383390" />

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Everything the trials reported">

### What the trials reported

- **Stomach and gut.** Nausea, diarrhoea, vomiting and constipation were the most common side effects, mostly mild to moderate and related to dose.<Cite id="jastreboff-2023-retatrutide-phase-2,rosenstock-2023-retatrutide-t2d-phase-2" /> In TRIUMPH-2, diarrhoea affected 27% to 34% against 13% on placebo, and nausea 14% to 28% against 8%.<Cite id="bellido-2026-triumph-2" />
- **Stopping.** In TRIUMPH-2, 4% to 12% stopped because of side effects or death, against 5% on placebo; in TRANSCEND-T2D-1, 2% to 5% against none.<Cite id="bellido-2026-triumph-2,bajaj-2026-transcend-t2d-1" />
- **Low blood pressure.** In TRIUMPH-2, 1% to 6% against fewer than 1% on placebo.<Cite id="bellido-2026-triumph-2" />
- **Abnormal skin sensations.** Burning, tingling or painful skin (dysaesthesia) affected 4% to 7% in TRIUMPH-2, against 1% on placebo.<Cite id="bellido-2026-triumph-2" /> In the phase 2 obesity trial, 4 of 62 people on the highest dose had allodynia, against none in any other group.<Cite id="ctgov-nct04881760" /> In the unpublished TRIUMPH-4, the company reported higher rates, up to about one in five at the highest dose; this figure has not been peer-reviewed. Most reported cases were mild to moderate, and the mechanism is unknown.<Cite id="kaczynski-2026-retatrutide-review" /> The EU product information for semaglutide (Wegovy) and tirzepatide (Mounjaro) also lists dysaesthesia, so it may not be unique to retatrutide.<Cite id="ema-epar-wegovy,ema-epar-mounjaro" />
- **Allergic-type reactions.** Pooling three early trials, they were more frequent than with placebo (risk ratio 3.79, 95% CI 1.20–11.96).<Cite id="pasqualotto-2024-retatrutide-meta-analysis" />
- **Pancreas and gallbladder.** A few serious cases of acute pancreatitis (three) and gallbladder inflammation (two) occurred across the two phase 2 trials.<Cite id="ctgov-nct04881760,ctgov-nct04867785" /> Both are known, uncommon risks of GLP-1-based drugs.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" />
- **Deaths.** TRIUMPH-2 recorded six deaths on retatrutide and one on placebo; the investigators judged all unrelated to the drug.<Cite id="bellido-2026-triumph-2" />

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Why a heart-rhythm effect is theorised">

1. **Retatrutide raises heart rate in people,** in a dose-related way.<Cite id="jastreboff-2023-retatrutide-phase-2" />
2. **In mice, the glucagon receptor speeds the pacemaker.** <Claim level="animal">In isolated mouse atria, retatrutide raised the beating rate through the glucagon receptor and cAMP; a glucagon-receptor blocker reduced this rise</Claim>.<Cite id="neumann-2025-retatrutide-mouse-atria" />
3. **In human heart muscle, it raises force.** <Claim level="in-vitro">In atrial tissue from 29 adults with coronary disease, retatrutide made the muscle contract harder, and blockers of each of its three receptors weakened this effect</Claim>.<Cite id="neumann-2026-retatrutide-human-atria" /> <Claim level="hypothesis">Whether this extra drive makes the heart more prone to rhythm problems in some people is the open question</Claim>.
4. **Early numbers point the same way, without proof.** <Claim level="human-controlled">Pooling three early trials, rhythm problems were about three times as frequent as with placebo, but not statistically significantly (risk ratio 3.41, 95% CI 0.86–13.55)</Claim>.<Cite id="pasqualotto-2024-retatrutide-meta-analysis" /> One person in the phase 2 diabetes trial had a serious episode of atrial fibrillation.<Cite id="ctgov-nct04867785" />

There are honest counter-arguments. A Spanish laboratory study found no glucagon receptors and no glucagon effect on rate or force in human heart tissue, unlike in rats.<Cite id="aranda-domene-2023-glucagon-human-heart" /> That conflicts with the German human-tissue study above, so the two laboratories disagree about glucagon receptors in the human heart.<Cite id="neumann-2026-retatrutide-human-atria" /> Weight loss itself may protect: <Claim level="animal">in obese mice, three weeks of retatrutide made atrial fibrillation harder to trigger</Claim>.<Cite id="takahashi-2026-af-mice" /> And the rise in heart rate faded after 24 weeks.<Cite id="jastreboff-2023-retatrutide-phase-2" /> The outcomes trial is designed to settle this.<Cite id="ctgov-nct06383390" />

</InDetail>

### Muscle and lean mass

<Claim level="human-limited">Across GLP-1-based medicines, lean mass has made up from about 15% to 40–60% of the weight lost</Claim>; lean mass includes organs and water as well as muscle, and MRI studies of these older drugs suggest the muscle change largely fits the weight lost. The source is a narrative review.<Cite id="neeland-2024-lean-mass" /> For retatrutide, the only human data are a company substudy with complete scans in 103 people.<Cite id="coskun-2025-retatrutide-body-composition" /> <Claim level="hypothesis">Because glucagon drives the breakdown of amino acids, a glucagon-receptor drug could take more lean tissue than other drugs</Claim>; an independent review lists this as a possible cost.<Cite id="elmendorf-2026-glucagon-iuphar-review" /> In mice, lean mass fell along with fat.<Cite id="briand-2026-retatrutide-mash-models,hitaka-2026-retatrutide-mc4r" /> Effects on strength and falls are unknown.

### Low blood sugar, and type 1 diabetes

No severe low blood sugar was reported in the phase 2 diabetes trial or in TRANSCEND-T2D-1.<Cite id="rosenstock-2023-retatrutide-t2d-phase-2,bajaj-2026-transcend-t2d-1" /> With insulin or sulfonylureas, which lower glucose whether it is high or not, other GLP-1-based drugs can push blood sugar too low.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" /> TRIUMPH-2 allowed sulfonylureas, but its abstract gives no figures for low blood sugar.<Cite id="bellido-2026-triumph-2" /> None of the 34 registered retatrutide studies is about type 1 diabetes.<Cite id="ctgov-retatrutide-search-2026-10-20" /> In one case report, a man with type 1 diabetes developed severe vomiting, high ketones and kidney injury after an online product, and later repeated low blood sugar; he also had a gut infection, so the cause is uncertain.<Cite id="branine-2026-retatrutide-ketosis-case" /> In type 1 diabetes, vomiting and missed insulin let ketones build up.<Cite id="branine-2026-retatrutide-ketosis-case" /> <Claim level="hypothesis">A drug that also raises ketones could make this worse</Claim>.<Cite id="sanyal-2024-retatrutide-masld" />

### Thyroid and pregnancy

<Claim level="animal">GLP-1 receptor agonists stimulate thyroid C-cells in rodents</Claim>; human and monkey C-cells carry few GLP-1 receptors, so the relevance to people is uncertain.<Cite id="knudsen-2010-thyroid-c-cells" /> The US labels of authorised drugs of this class warn about medullary thyroid cancer.<Cite id="fda-label-mounjaro" /> TRIUMPH-1 excluded people with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN-2.<Cite id="ctgov-nct05929066" /> We found no published carcinogenicity or reproduction studies of retatrutide.

<InDetail title="What happens outside trials, and in sport">

### What happens outside trials

- **Wrong amounts.** Three products sold as <Q>10 mg</Q> in Australia held 51%, 165% and 190% of the label; a trace of lead was well below safety limits, and none was tested for sterility.<Cite id="piatkowski-2026-retatrutide-product-testing" />
- **A different drug.** In Australian testing, one product sold as retatrutide held "a high dose of semaglutide and no retatrutide", JAMA reported.<Cite id="rubin-2026-jama-news" />
- **Liver injury.** The Victoria Department of Health in Australia received reports of six cases of liver toxicity linked to products labelled retatrutide.<Cite id="rubin-2026-jama-news" /> No sign of liver toxicity was seen in the phase 2 obesity trial,<Cite id="sanyal-2024-retatrutide-masld" /> so <Claim level="hypothesis">a contaminant may be to blame</Claim>; this has not been shown.
- **Hospital care.** A young woman was admitted to hospital with days of vomiting after injections given by a non-medical "life coach".<Cite id="buzzi-2026-retatrutide-case-report" />
- **Self-reports.** 13,589 social media users wrote about using it. Among the 7,823 who named a symptom, common ones were increased appetite, fatigue, increased energy, nausea and insomnia; 4.8% mentioned a raised heart rate. These are posts, not measurements.<Cite id="sehgal-2026-retatrutide-social-media" />

<Lead question="How can someone lower the risk?">Ask about a trial or an approved medicine. Treat any online product as unchecked. Know when to stop.</Lead>

### The legal routes

In the EU, retatrutide is in practice legal only inside a trial. You can look for recruiting trials in the EU and US trial registers and ask your doctor whether you might qualify; trials have strict entry rules.<Cite id="ctgov-nct06383390,ctgov-nct07165028" />

Two related medicines are authorised: [semaglutide](/en/compounds/semaglutide/) and [tirzepatide](/en/compounds/tirzepatide/). They come with a label, a known dose and medical follow-up. A doctor can tell you whether one of them suits you. See [talking to your doctor](/en/talking-to-your-doctor/) and [legal routes](/en/legal-routes/).

### When to stop and get help

<Callout type="stop" title="Get medical help now" id="stop-now">
- **Severe, constant pain in the upper belly**, often spreading to the back, with or without vomiting: possible pancreatitis. Stop and get urgent care.
- **A racing, pounding or irregular heartbeat with dizziness, fainting, chest pain or breathlessness:** call your local emergency number.
- **Yellow skin or eyes, dark urine, or easy bruising:** possible liver injury. Stop and get same-day care.
- **Vomiting so much you cannot keep fluids down, or passing very little urine:** risk of dehydration and kidney injury. Get same-day care.
- **Swelling of the face, lips or throat, or difficulty breathing:** possible allergic reaction. Call your local emergency number.
- **With type 1 diabetes: vomiting, fast breathing or high ketones:** possible ketoacidosis. Get emergency care.
</Callout>

Burning, tingling or painful skin is not an emergency, but it is a reason to stop and see a doctor. In the trials most cases were mild to moderate; the cause is not known.<Cite id="kaczynski-2026-retatrutide-review" />

<InDetail title="What you cannot know about an online product">

You cannot see what is in a vial or pen. Tested products held from half to nearly twice the amount on the label, and one held semaglutide instead.<Cite id="piatkowski-2026-retatrutide-product-testing,rubin-2026-jama-news" /> No published study has tested them for sterility. See [product risk](/en/safety/product-risk/). If you inject anything, follow [sterile technique](/en/safety/sterile-technique/): a dirty product or needle can cause an infection.

About half of an injected dose is still in the body six days later.<Cite id="urva-2022-ly3437943-phase-1b" /> So if you get a side effect, stopping will not end it the next day.

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Checks that make sense, and why">

Discuss these with a doctor, who decides what is right for you. Tell them what you used.

<Checklist>

- **Pulse and heart rhythm** at rest, because retatrutide raises heart rate and a rhythm effect has not been ruled out. A doctor can arrange an ECG (heart tracing) if you feel palpitations.
- **Liver tests**, because of the Australian reports of liver injury with products of unknown content.
- **Blood glucose**, if you use insulin or a sulfonylurea (such as gliclazide), because the combination could push sugar too low.
- **Ketones**, if you have type 1 diabetes, especially when you are vomiting or cannot eat.
- **Kidney function** after days of vomiting or diarrhoea, because losing fluid can harm the kidneys.

</Checklist>

<InDetail title="Interactions, and who should not use it at all">

### Plausible interactions (none published)

- **Insulin and sulfonylureas:** higher risk of low blood sugar, as with other GLP-1-based drugs.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" />
- **The contraceptive pill:** a short company report says retatrutide delays stomach emptying (we could read only its title and index terms).<Cite id="urva-2023-retatrutide-gastric-emptying" /> <Claim level="hypothesis">Slower stomach emptying could change how well tablets are absorbed</Claim>. A pill study finished in 2024 without public results.<Cite id="ctgov-nct06039826" /> For tirzepatide, UK and US regulators advise a non-pill method or condoms for four weeks after starting and after each dose increase.<Cite id="mhra-glp1-what-you-need-to-know,fda-label-mounjaro" />
- **Warfarin and other medicines where small changes matter:** an interaction study finished in 2023 without published results.<Cite id="ctgov-nct05445232" />
- **Other GLP-1 drugs, or "stacks" with other peptides:** they add the same side effects, and make any reaction harder to trace.

### Who should not use it at all

- **Anyone pregnant, breastfeeding or trying to conceive:** there are no human data, and the authorised drugs of this class harmed unborn animals in studies.<Cite id="ema-epar-wegovy,ema-epar-mounjaro" />
- **Anyone who has had pancreatitis**, or with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN-2: TRIUMPH-1 excluded these people.<Cite id="ctgov-nct05929066" />
- **Anyone with a heart rhythm problem or heart failure**, because of the rise in heart rate and the open rhythm question.<Cite id="jastreboff-2023-retatrutide-phase-2,pasqualotto-2024-retatrutide-meta-analysis" />
- **People with type 1 diabetes**, because of the ketone risk described above.
- **Children and teenagers**: all published trials were in adults.<Cite id="jastreboff-2026-triumph-1,bajaj-2026-transcend-t2d-1" />
- **Athletes subject to anti-doping rules**: it is prohibited at all times and detectable for weeks.<Cite id="wada-2026-list-en,carneiro-2026-retatrutide-antidoping" />

</InDetail>
