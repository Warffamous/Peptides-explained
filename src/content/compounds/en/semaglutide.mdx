<InDetail title="The approved products, and why this page looks different">

Semaglutide is a prescription medicine made by Novo Nordisk. It is sold as Ozempic (a weekly injection for type 2 diabetes), Rybelsus (a daily tablet for type 2 diabetes) and Wegovy (for weight management: a weekly injection and, in the US and the UK, also a daily tablet). <InDetail title="The molecule, where it came from, and the tablet">

### What the molecule is

Semaglutide is a chain of 31 amino acids. Twenty-nine are the same as in human GLP-1 (7–37), the active form of a hormone the gut releases after a meal; the labels call this 94% sequence homology.<Cite id="fda-label-wegovy,ema-epar-wegovy" /> The chain starts <Seq>His-Aib-Glu-Gly-Thr-Phe</Seq>.

Three changes make the hormone last a week. Positions use the GLP-1 (7–37) numbering of the literature and the US label, in which the first histidine is position 7.<Cite id="fda-label-ozempic,lau-2015-semaglutide-discovery,pubchem-semaglutide" />

- **Position 8:** alanine is replaced by Aib (2-aminoisobutyric acid), which stabilises the peptide against the enzyme DPP-4.
- **Position 34:** lysine is replaced by arginine, so only one lysine is left for the side chain.
- **Position 26:** that lysine carries two short hydrophilic spacers, a glutamic-acid linker and an 18-carbon fatty diacid. This part binds to albumin in the blood.

The formula is C187H291N45O59 and the molecular mass <Q>4113.58 g/mol</Q>; the US label states that the peptide backbone is produced by yeast fermentation.<Cite id="fda-label-ozempic" />


### Where it came from

In 1987 three groups showed that a gut fragment of proglucagon releases insulin: in the pig pancreas, the rat pancreas and <Claim level="human-limited">seven human volunteers, in whom an infusion raised insulin and lowered glucose and glucagon</Claim>.<Cite id="holst-1987-glp1,mojsov-1987-glp1,kreymann-1987-glp1" /> In 1993 <Claim level="human-limited">a GLP-1 infusion normalised fasting glucose in 10 people with type 2 diabetes, and insulin fell back once glucose was normal</Claim>.<Cite id="nauck-1993-glp1-type2" /> But the natural hormone lasts about 1–2 minutes, because DPP-4 cuts it and the kidneys clear it.<Cite id="muller-2019-glp1-review" /> In a study of 8 people with type 2 diabetes and 8 healthy volunteers, most of the GLP-1 measured in blood 30 minutes after an injection under the skin was already the cut, largely inactive form.<Cite id="deacon-1995-glp1-degradation,muller-2019-glp1-review" /> An earlier analogue, liraglutide, used a fatty-acid side chain and was given daily; semaglutide was designed for stronger albumin binding and full stability against breakdown.<Cite id="lau-2015-semaglutide-discovery" />

### Injection and tablet

Peptides are digested in the gut, so most are injected. Semaglutide tablets contain an absorption enhancer, SNAC (salcaprozate sodium); <Claim level="human-limited">in dogs and people, absorption took place in the stomach, next to the dissolving tablet</Claim>.<Cite id="buckley-2018-oral-semaglutide" /> Only about 1–2% of the dose is absorbed, varying widely between people, so tablet strengths are much higher than injection strengths.<Cite id="fda-label-wegovy,ema-epar-rybelsus" />

The European label (EMA product information) states that the effect of switching between tablets and injections cannot easily be predicted.<Cite id="ema-epar-rybelsus" />

<InDetail title="Why “semaglutide” is not one thing">

The evidence on this page belongs to manufactured products: a known substance in a pen or tablet that delivers a fixed amount, with a label updated as new risks are found. A vial sold online as "research semaglutide" shares the name, but none of the guarantees that let that evidence apply: known content, a fixed dose, sterility and an updated label.<Cite id="fda-unapproved-glp1-concerns" />

Lumping authorised GLP-1 medicines together with grey-market peptides misleads in two directions:

- **It flatters the experimental peptides.** "Ozempic works and it is a peptide, so the other peptides probably work too." Semaglutide's evidence comes from its own trials in tens of thousands of people, not from being a peptide; most peptides sold alongside it have no comparable trial.
- **It undersells the medicine and oversells the vial.** "It is all grey-market peptide stuff" can put people off a medicine that, in specific groups, lowered the combined risk of heart attack, stroke or cardiovascular death, and of serious kidney events, in large trials, while inviting them to treat an online vial as if it carried that safety record. It does not.

### What each step rests on

**Staying in the blood.** The labels give a half-life of about one week and more than 99% albumin binding, measured in people.<Cite id="fda-label-wegovy,ema-epar-wegovy" /> The chain is mechanical: albumin is too big for the kidneys to filter, so only the small free fraction is cleared, and Aib at position 8 protects against DPP-4. Semaglutide therefore stays in the circulation for about five to seven weeks after the last dose; side effects do not end the day you stop.<Cite id="fda-label-wegovy,ema-epar-wegovy,ema-epar-rybelsus" />

**Receptor and cAMP.** Semaglutide is a direct agonist at the GLP-1 receptor, with an affinity measured in cell systems.<Cite id="chembl-semaglutide,lau-2015-semaglutide-discovery" /> The activated receptor switches on adenylate cyclase, so cAMP rises inside the cell; in beta cells, cAMP acts through PKA and Epac2 to increase insulin release.<Cite id="muller-2019-glp1-review" /> <Claim level="in-vitro">This chain is established in cell lines and animal islets</Claim>, not traced step by step in human pancreas.

**Why the insulin effect depends on glucose.** cAMP amplifies an insulin release that glucose has already triggered; it does not start one. <Claim level="human-controlled">In a 12-week, manufacturer-funded, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 75 people with type 2 diabetes, semaglutide increased insulin secretion and lowered glucagon after meals</Claim>.<Cite id="kapitza-2017-beta-cell" /> The labels add that during low blood sugar, insulin release still falls and the glucagon response is not impaired.<Cite id="fda-label-ozempic,ema-epar-wegovy" /> That is why its own risk of low blood sugar is low, and why much of that protection is lost when it is combined with medicines that raise insulin regardless of glucose.

**Appetite.** <Claim level="animal">In rodent experiments by the manufacturer, semaglutide reached the brainstem and hypothalamus through regions where the blood–brain barrier is open, and activated areas thought to be involved in ending a meal</Claim>.<Cite id="gabery-2020-semaglutide-brain" /> <Claim level="human-controlled">In a manufacturer-funded, double-blind, randomised trial in 72 adults with obesity, 20 weeks of semaglutide cut energy intake at a free-choice lunch by 35% compared with placebo, with less hunger and fewer cravings</Claim>.<Cite id="friedrichsen-2021-appetite" /> The eating effect is measured in people; the brain sites are inferred from animals.

**The stomach.** GLP-1 slows stomach emptying.<Cite id="muller-2019-glp1-review" /> The labels describe a minor delay early after a meal, and no clear effect in a study at the <Q>2.4 mg</Q> strength; the appetite trial found no clear delay at week 20.<Cite id="fda-label-ozempic,ema-epar-wegovy,friedrichsen-2021-appetite" />

The European label (EMA product information) gives tolerance as the probable reason.<Cite id="ema-epar-wegovy" />

The heart and kidney benefits are real in the populations studied but only partly explained. The US label states that "the exact mechanism of semaglutide in CV risk reduction in adults has not been established".<Cite id="fda-label-wegovy" />

<InDetail title="Diabetes, the heart and the kidneys">

### Type 2 diabetes and the heart

<Claim level="established">Semaglutide lowers HbA1c in type 2 diabetes</Claim> (in PIONEER 1, a double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 3 trial in 703 adults, at all three tablet strengths over 26 weeks).<Cite id="aroda-2019-pioneer-1" /> But lower glucose does not by itself mean fewer heart attacks. SUSTAIN-6 randomised 3,297 people with type 2 diabetes at high cardiovascular risk to weekly semaglutide (<Q>0.5 mg</Q> or <Q>1.0 mg</Q>) or placebo for 104 weeks: cardiovascular death, non-fatal heart attack or non-fatal stroke occurred in 6.6% versus 8.9% (HR 0.74, 95% CI 0.58–0.95).<Cite id="marso-2016-sustain-6" /> It was designed to rule out harm, so the favourable result was encouraging rather than conclusive. PIONEER 6 did the same check for the tablet in 3,183 people over a median 15.9 months (3.8% versus 4.8%; HR 0.79, 95% CI 0.57–1.11): no large excess risk, but no proof of benefit.<Cite id="husain-2019-pioneer-6" />

SOUL then tested benefit directly in 9,650 people with type 2 diabetes and atherosclerotic disease, chronic kidney disease or both, over a mean 47.5 months: <Claim level="human-controlled">major cardiovascular events occurred in 12.0% with oral semaglutide and 13.8% with placebo (HR 0.86, 95% CI 0.77–0.96)</Claim>; the confirmatory secondary outcomes, including a kidney composite, did not differ significantly.<Cite id="mcguire-2025-soul" />

### Kidneys

FLOW randomised 3,533 people with type 2 diabetes and chronic kidney disease to weekly semaglutide or placebo (median follow-up 3.4 years). <Claim level="human-controlled">Kidney failure, a fall in kidney function of at least half, or kidney or cardiovascular death was 24% less frequent with semaglutide (HR 0.76, 95% CI 0.66–0.88)</Claim>.<Cite id="perkovic-2024-flow" /> It stopped early at a prespecified interim analysis. Trials stopped early for benefit tend to overstate the effect, although large overestimates are mainly seen in trials with fewer than 500 events; FLOW had 741.<Cite id="bassler-2010-truncated-trials,perkovic-2024-flow" />

### Heart outcomes without diabetes

SELECT randomised 17,604 adults with cardiovascular disease and overweight or obesity, but no diabetes, for a mean 39.8 months. <Claim level="human-controlled">Cardiovascular death, non-fatal heart attack or non-fatal stroke occurred in 6.5% with semaglutide and 8.0% with placebo (HR 0.80, 95% CI 0.72–0.90)</Claim>; 16.6% stopped because of adverse events, against 8.2% on placebo.<Cite id="lincoff-2023-select" /> It does not show that semaglutide prevents a first heart attack in people without heart or vessel disease.

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Weight management, and what happens after stopping">

STEP 1 randomised 1,961 adults with obesity, or overweight with a weight-related condition, and no diabetes, to weekly semaglutide (<Q>2.4 mg</Q>) or placebo for 68 weeks, with lifestyle support. <Claim level="established">Mean weight fell by 14.9% versus 2.4%</Claim>; half of the semaglutide group lost 15% or more, while about one in seven lost less than 5%.<Cite id="wilding-2021-step-1" />

<Claim level="human-controlled">Much of the effect is lost when treatment stops</Claim>. In STEP 4, a double-blind phase 3 trial, 803 people who had reached the target dose during a 20-week run-in on semaglutide (average loss 10.6%) were randomised to continue (a further 7.9% lost over 48 weeks) or switch to placebo (6.9% regained).<Cite id="rubino-2021-step-4" /> In the STEP 1 extension, of 327 people followed for a year after stopping, those who had taken semaglutide regained about two-thirds of the weight they had lost (a net loss of 5.6% remained), and most measures of blood pressure, lipids and glucose moved back towards baseline.<Cite id="wilding-2022-step-1-extension" />

The daily tablet for weight management was tested in OASIS 4, a manufacturer-funded, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 307 adults without diabetes: mean weight change at 64 weeks was −13.6% versus −2.2%, with gastrointestinal adverse events in 74.0% versus 42.2%.<Cite id="wharton-2025-oasis-4" /> The US label describes the same trial (as "Study 7") for Wegovy tablets.<Cite id="fda-label-wegovy" />

The European Medicines Agency cites it as a phase 3 trial.<Cite id="ema-2026-wegovy-tablets" />

Part D plans can still cover semaglutide for its other approved uses, such as type 2 diabetes.<Cite id="us-usc-42-1395w-102-e,us-usc-42-1396r-8-d,us-medicare-gov-weight-loss-drugs" /> From 1 July 2026 to 31 December 2027 a temporary Medicare programme, the Medicare GLP-1 Bridge, covers Wegovy (injection or tablets) for weight management for eligible people with Part D, with a $50 monthly copayment and prior authorisation. **United Kingdom.** On the NHS in England, NICE recommends Wegovy for weight management only within specialist weight management services, for up to 2 years, for adults with at least one weight-related condition and a BMI of 35 or more (or 30 to 34.9 if they meet the criteria for specialist referral).<Cite id="nice-ta875-semaglutide" /> Since May 2026 NICE also says it can be used to lower the risk of heart attack and stroke in adults with heart or blood-vessel disease and a BMI of at least 27; a review that covers the higher-strength pen is under way.<Cite id="nice-ta1152-semaglutide-mace,nice-id6699-semaglutide-obesity-review" /> In Scotland the Scottish Medicines Consortium accepted Wegovy pens for restricted use, but Wegovy tablets are not recommended there because the company has not applied.<Cite id="smc-2497-semaglutide-wegovy,smc-2988-semaglutide-wegovy-tablets" /> Private prescriptions are possible after a consultation with a prescriber.<Cite id="mhra-glp1-what-you-need-to-know" /> We did not review NHS rules for semaglutide in type 2 diabetes for this page.

**Canada.** Public drug plans negotiate prices together through the pCPA (pan-Canadian Pharmaceutical Alliance), and each province or territory then decides what its plan covers. The pCPA reached agreements for Ozempic and Rybelsus for type 2 diabetes.<Cite id="ca-pcpa-negotiations-2026-10-24,ca-pcpa-role-system" /> For Wegovy in weight management, Canada's Drug Agency advised in 2022 that public plans should not pay. In July 2025 it advised paying only for adults with a BMI of 27 or more who already have heart or blood-vessel disease, and only at a lower price. The price negotiation that followed ended without agreement in December 2025.<Cite id="ca-cda-amc-2022-wegovy,ca-cda-amc-2025-wegovy,ca-pcpa-negotiations-2026-10-24" />

**Australia.** The Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme (PBS) subsidises semaglutide only as Ozempic for type 2 diabetes, with an authority from the prescriber, since 1 July 2020. Wegovy is not on the PBS, so it is paid privately.<Cite id="au-pbs-semaglutide-2026-10-24,au-pbs-dusc-2024-06-semaglutide" /> The committee that advises on PBS listings turned down Wegovy for obesity in 2022 and 2023. In November 2025 it recommended Wegovy for people with obesity who already have heart or blood-vessel disease, but as of October 2026 that listing has not been made.<Cite id="au-pbs-medicine-status-wegovy,au-pbac-2025-11-obesity-treatments-advice" />

These gaps have costs, and the reasons for them are real too. A weight-management medicine that lowered heart events in people with heart disease in a large trial is out of reach for many people who cannot pay privately; whether this pushes people towards online products has not been measured.<Cite id="lincoff-2023-select" /> The payers' reasons include cost, and, for weight management, little evidence in the earlier trials of health gain beyond weight.<Cite id="ca-cda-amc-2022-wegovy,ca-cda-amc-2025-wegovy" />

<Lead question="What are the risks?">Many people feel sick at first. Gallstones, an inflamed pancreas and a rare eye condition are rarer.</Lead>

Unless stated otherwise, the figures come from the trials named and from the Wegovy product information assessed by the European Medicines Agency (EMA), which gives the trial results in detail.

<InDetail title="Stomach and gut: the exact numbers">

In the Wegovy weight trials (STEP 1–4, 68 weeks), nausea occurred in 43.9% with semaglutide versus 16.1% with placebo, diarrhoea in 29.7% versus 15.9%, vomiting in 24.5% versus 6.3% and constipation in 24.2% versus 11.1%. Most episodes were mild to moderate and came while the dose was being raised; 4.3% stopped treatment permanently because of them. Dehydration from vomiting or diarrhoea has rarely worsened kidney function, and intestinal obstruction has been reported after authorisation.<Cite id="ema-epar-wegovy" />

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Gallbladder and pancreas">

Gallstones were reported in 1.6% versus 1.1%, and gallbladder inflammation in 0.6% versus 0.3%.<Cite id="ema-epar-wegovy" /> <Claim level="human-controlled">A meta-analysis of 76 randomised trials of GLP-1 medicines (103,371 participants) found more gallbladder and bile-duct disease (relative risk 1.37), more so in weight-loss trials, at higher doses and with longer use</Claim>.<Cite id="he-2022-gallbladder" /> Adjudicated acute pancreatitis occurred in 0.2% versus less than 0.1% in the weight trials, and 0.2% versus 0.3% in SELECT; semaglutide should be stopped if pancreatitis is suspected and not restarted if it is confirmed.<Cite id="ema-epar-wegovy" />

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Eyes: diabetic retinopathy and NAION">

### Eyes: diabetic retinopathy

In SUSTAIN-6, diabetic retinopathy complications (bleeding in the eye, blindness, or the need for laser or eye injections) occurred in 3.0% versus 1.8% (HR 1.76), mainly in insulin-treated people with existing retinopathy.<Cite id="marso-2016-sustain-6,ema-epar-wegovy" /> <Claim level="human-limited">A manufacturer-funded post hoc analysis of SUSTAIN-6 suggested that most of the excess may be due to the size and speed of the fall in HbA1c in the first 16 weeks</Claim>, an "early worsening" also seen with insulin.<Cite id="vilsboll-2018-retinopathy" /> The label notes that other mechanisms cannot be excluded.<Cite id="ema-epar-wegovy" />

### Eyes: NAION

{/* residue-ok: EMA evidence */}
The safety committee of the European Medicines Agency (PRAC) classes NAION as very rare (up to 1 in 10,000 people): <Claim level="human-limited">large epidemiological studies in type 2 diabetes suggest roughly a doubling of risk, about one extra case per 10,000 person-years</Claim>.<Cite id="ema-prac-2025-06-naion" /> An earlier retrospective single-centre study of 1,689 matched patients found much higher hazard ratios (4.28 and 7.64), with few events and wide confidence intervals, in patients referred to a specialist eye service.<Cite id="hathaway-2024-naion" />
{/* residue-ok: EMA evidence */}
Neither that committee's announcement nor the European label proposes a mechanism.<Cite id="ema-prac-2025-06-naion,ema-epar-wegovy" /> The UK regulator also warns of very rare NAION with semaglutide; the US labels we checked do not list it.<Cite id="mhra-glp1-what-you-need-to-know,fda-label-wegovy,fda-label-ozempic" />

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Low blood sugar, with a short animation">

Semaglutide's own risk of hypoglycaemia is low because its insulin effect depends on glucose, but it is not zero: in STEP 2, a weight trial in people with type 2 diabetes, clinically significant low blood sugar occurred in 6.2% with semaglutide versus 2.5% with placebo, with and without a sulfonylurea. Insulin and sulfonylureas act regardless of glucose, so in combination blood sugar can fall too low; the labels say this risk can be lowered by reducing the dose of those medicines.<Cite id="ema-epar-wegovy,fda-label-wegovy" />

The animation shows how the combination pushes blood sugar too low, and what to do about it.

<Explainer id="insulin-glp1-hypo" />

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Thyroid tumours in rodents, and why they may not carry over">

In two-year studies, semaglutide caused thyroid C-cell tumours in rats and mice at clinically relevant exposures.<Cite id="ema-epar-wegovy,fda-label-ozempic" /> <Claim level="animal">Rodent C-cells carry GLP-1 receptors, and GLP-1 agonists stimulated calcitonin release and, over time, C-cell hyperplasia in rats and, less, in mice</Claim>. <Claim level="animal">Human and monkey C-cells had low receptor levels, the agonists did not switch on adenylate cyclase or calcitonin release in primates, and 20 months of liraglutide caused no C-cell hyperplasia in monkeys</Claim>.<Cite id="knudsen-2010-thyroid-c-cells" /> The US and Canadian labels say the human relevance has not been determined.<Cite id="fda-label-wegovy,ca-pm-wegovy-2026-10-02" />
{/* residue-ok: EMA evidence */}
The European label rates it as low but not excluded.<Cite id="ema-epar-wegovy" />

Human data are mixed: a French case-control study linked one to three years of GLP-1 medicines with more thyroid cancer, including medullary cancer, while a larger Scandinavian cohort found no clear increase over a mean 3.9 years but could not rule out a modest one. Both analysed GLP-1 medicines as a class rather than semaglutide alone; in the Scandinavian study, semaglutide made up about a third of use.<Cite id="bezin-2023-thyroid-cancer,pasternak-2024-thyroid-cancer" /> <Claim level="hypothesis">More thyroid check-ups in people on these medicines would find more small cancers and could inflate an observed risk</Claim>. The US and Canadian contraindication covers a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma, or MEN 2, where the baseline risk is already high.<Cite id="fda-label-ozempic,ca-pm-ozempic-2026-07-13" />

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Anaesthesia, muscle, weight regain, mood and pregnancy">

### Anaesthesia, muscle and weight regain

Slower stomach emptying means food may remain in the stomach during general anaesthesia or deep sedation, and could be breathed into the lungs; cases have been reported, so tell the doctor about semaglutide before a procedure.<Cite id="ema-prac-2024-07-aspiration,ema-epar-wegovy,fda-label-wegovy,mhra-glp1-what-you-need-to-know" />
{/* residue-ok: EMA evidence */}
The European Medicines Agency's safety committee could not establish that GLP-1 medicines cause this (July 2024), but asked for the warning in the product information.<Cite id="ema-prac-2024-07-aspiration" />

<Claim level="human-limited">In trials of GLP-1-based medicines, lean mass has made up from about 15% to 40–60% of the weight lost</Claim>. Lean mass also includes organs, bone and water, and MRI studies suggest the muscle change may fit the weight lost, but older or frail people may lose function; the source is a narrative review and the question is open.<Cite id="neeland-2024-lean-mass" />

In the STEP 1 extension, about two-thirds of the lost weight returned within a year of stopping, and most gains in blood pressure, lipids and glucose faded; the STEP 1 investigators see this as confirming that obesity is chronic.<Cite id="wilding-2022-step-1-extension,rubino-2021-step-4" />

### Mood, pregnancy and other effects

The UK regulator, after a full safety review, concluded that the data do not support a causal link between GLP-1 medicines and depression or suicidal thoughts, and the US Wegovy label dropped its suicidal-behaviour warning in early 2026.<Cite id="mhra-glp1-what-you-need-to-know,fda-label-wegovy" />
{/* residue-ok: EMA evidence */}
The European Medicines Agency's safety committee reached the same conclusion in 2024. If your mood changes on any medicine, tell your doctor.<Cite id="ema-prac-2024-04-suicidal-thoughts" />

In rats semaglutide harmed embryos, partly through a yolk-sac mechanism thought not to apply to humans; rabbits and monkeys had more pregnancy loss with marked maternal weight loss. The labels say not to use it in pregnancy or while breastfeeding, and to stop at least two months before a planned pregnancy.<Cite id="ema-epar-wegovy,fda-label-wegovy,mhra-glp1-what-you-need-to-know" /> Weight loss can improve fertility and so raise the chance of an unplanned pregnancy; a US review reports such pregnancies and advises contraception while taking these medicines.<Cite id="drummond-2024-glp1-pregnancy-review" />

The label also lists a rise in heart rate (on average about 3 beats per minute), hair loss (2.5% versus 1.0%), headache, dizziness, tiredness, rare allergic reactions including anaphylaxis and angioedema, and, mainly at <Q>7.2 mg</Q>, altered skin sensation (21.6% versus 0.3% in STEP UP).<Cite id="ema-epar-wegovy" />

A regulator assessed what is in it, the pen or tablet fixes the amount, and the maker releases each batch.<Cite id="fda-drugsatfda-ozempic,fda-drugsatfda-wegovy,ema-epar-ozempic,ema-epar-rybelsus,ema-epar-wegovy" /> This section gives no doses: your prescriber decides the dose. The checks below are for any other product, such as an online "research" vial or a pen of unknown origin.

### When to stop and get help

<Callout type="stop" title="Get medical help now" id="stop-now">
- **Severe, constant pain in the upper belly**, often into the back, with or without vomiting: possible pancreatitis. Stop semaglutide and get urgent care.
- **Sudden loss of vision or rapidly worsening eyesight, often in one eye:** possible NAION. Contact a doctor without delay.
- **Shaking, sweating, confusion or drowsiness after injecting a product from outside a pharmacy:** possibly insulin in a fake. Take something sugary if you can swallow safely; call your local emergency number if symptoms are severe or the person becomes drowsy or confused.
- **Swelling of the face, lips or throat, or difficulty breathing:** possible allergic reaction. Call your local emergency number.
- **Vomiting so much you cannot keep fluids down, or passing very little urine:** risk of dehydration and kidney injury. Get same-day care.

For an approved medicine, the legal route has the fewest unknowns.

### The legal route

In the US, the UK, Canada and Australia, semaglutide is a prescription medicine: a doctor or another prescriber decides, and a pharmacy supplies it.<Cite id="fda-unapproved-glp1-concerns,mhra-glp1-what-you-need-to-know,ca-hc-2026-01-glp1-advisory,au-poisons-standard-2026-10" /> The UK regulator explains why the consultation comes first: so that the prescriber can check that the medicine suits you and explain its benefits and risks.<Cite id="mhra-glp1-what-you-need-to-know" /> Who pays differs by country (see "Who pays" in the status section above). See [talking to your doctor](/en/talking-to-your-doctor/).

The [treatment routes](/en/legal-routes/) page explains each legal route and what it needs: [a prescription](/en/legal-routes/#prescription), [off-label use](/en/legal-routes/#off-label), [US pharmacy compounding](/en/legal-routes/#us-compounding) (now narrow for semaglutide, because the US shortage is over), [importing for yourself](/en/legal-routes/#personal-import) and [clinical trials](/en/legal-routes/#trials).<Cite id="fda-off-label-use,fda-glp1-compounding-policy" /> The routes for medicines that are not approved or not available, such as [UK "specials"](/en/legal-routes/#uk-specials), [Canada's Special Access Program](/en/legal-routes/#canada-special-access) and [Australia's Special Access Scheme](/en/legal-routes/#australia-sas), do not fit here: semaglutide is approved in all four countries, and the UK regulator says a licensed product should be used whenever it can meet the need.<Cite id="mhra-gn14-specials,ca-hc-sap-drugs,au-tga-sas-individual-patients" />

### Why an online "research" vial is a different product

The FDA has warned companies selling semaglutide falsely labelled "for research purposes" or "not for human consumption" to consumers, and calls such products of unknown quality. It also warns that some compounded products use salt forms (semaglutide sodium or acetate), which are different active ingredients from the one in the approved medicines.<Cite id="fda-unapproved-glp1-concerns" /> The MHRA states that powder-in-vial GLP-1 products are not authorised.<Cite id="mhra-glp1-what-you-need-to-know" /> Health Canada warns that unauthorised or fake GLP-1 products can hold too much, too little or none of the active ingredient, as well as unlisted ingredients or contaminants, and that injecting them can cause infection.<Cite id="ca-hc-2026-01-glp1-advisory" /> See [product risk](/en/safety/product-risk/).

Vials also bring dosing errors that pens prevent. The FDA has reports of people taking five to 20 times the intended amount from multi-dose vials of compounded semaglutide, often from confusing milligrams, millilitres and syringe "units". Some needed hospital care; with a one-week half-life, overdose may need prolonged observation and treatment.<Cite id="fda-2024-compounded-semaglutide-dosing-errors" /> For multi-dose vials of compounded GLP-1 medicines, the FDA advises a new sterile needle and syringe every time, discarding a vial 28 days after first use because contamination risk rises after that, and not using a product that arrived warm.<Cite id="fda-unapproved-glp1-concerns" /> See [sterile technique](/en/safety/sterile-technique/).

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Who should not use it, interactions, and useful checks">

### Who should not use it, and interactions

- **Pregnancy, trying to conceive, breastfeeding:** animal harm to the embryo; stop at least two months before a planned pregnancy.<Cite id="ema-epar-wegovy" />
- **Personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma, or MEN 2:** a contraindication on the US and Canadian labels, because of the rodent finding; discuss it with your prescriber wherever you live.<Cite id="fda-label-ozempic,ca-pm-ozempic-2026-07-13" />
- **Previous pancreatitis:** use with caution, because GLP-1 medicines are linked to acute pancreatitis. **Severe gastroparesis:** not recommended, because semaglutide slows the stomach further. **Uncontrolled or unstable diabetic retinopathy:** not recommended, because there is no experience in this group and a fast fall in glucose can temporarily worsen retinopathy. **Type 1 diabetes:** not studied; semaglutide never replaces insulin.<Cite id="ema-epar-wegovy" />
- **Insulin or sulfonylureas (such as gliclazide):** higher risk of low blood sugar. **Other GLP-1 medicines:** do not combine.<Cite id="ema-epar-wegovy" />
- **Tablets that need fast or precise absorption:** slower stomach emptying can change absorption; with semaglutide tablets levothyroxine exposure rose by 33%.<Cite id="ema-epar-wegovy,ema-epar-rybelsus" />
- **Warfarin or other coumarins, such as acenocoumarol:** cases of lower INR have been reported with acenocoumarol; the label advises frequent INR checks when starting.<Cite id="ema-epar-wegovy" />

### Checks that make sense, and why

- **Eyes**, if you have diabetic retinopathy: a fast fall in glucose can temporarily worsen it, and the label asks for close monitoring.<Cite id="vilsboll-2018-retinopathy,ema-epar-wegovy" />
- **Blood glucose**, if you also use insulin or a sulfonylurea; **INR** on coumarins; **thyroid tests** on levothyroxine (see above).
- **Kidney function** after prolonged vomiting or diarrhoea, because dehydration can harm the kidneys.<Cite id="ema-epar-wegovy" />
