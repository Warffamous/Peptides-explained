<Section id="what-it-is">

<AuthorisedProducts />

Tirzepatide is a prescription medicine made by Eli Lilly. In the EU it has been authorised as Mounjaro since 15 September 2022 for type 2 diabetes in adults; weight management in adults was added on 11 December 2023, and type 2 diabetes from age 10 on 22 January 2026.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro,ema-mounjaro-procedural-steps-archive,ema-mounjaro-procedural-steps" /> In the US the same molecule is marketed as Mounjaro for diabetes (approved 13 May 2022) and as Zepbound for weight management (approved 8 November 2023; sleep apnoea added on 20 December 2024).<Cite id="fda-drugsatfda-mounjaro,fda-drugsatfda-zepbound" />

<Callout type="note" title="Why this page looks different">
Most compounds on this site are experimental. Tirzepatide is an authorised medicine with large trials behind it. It is here because vials sold online as "research tirzepatide" use its name, and because grouping it with experimental peptides misleads in both directions.
</Callout>

### What the molecule is

Tirzepatide is a chain of 39 amino acids. The US label describes it as based on the sequence of GIP (glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide), a hormone released by the upper small intestine after a meal.<Cite id="fda-label-mounjaro,coskun-2018-ly3298176" /> The chain starts <Seq>Tyr-Aib-Glu-Gly-Thr-Phe</Seq>. Three changes stand out:<Cite id="fda-label-mounjaro,coskun-2018-ly3298176,pubchem-tirzepatide" />

- **Positions 2 and 13:** Aib (2-aminoisobutyric acid), an amino acid that the body does not use to build proteins.
- **Position 20:** a lysine carrying two short spacers, a glutamic-acid linker and a 20-carbon fatty diacid. This tail binds to albumin in the blood.
- **The end of the chain** is an amide rather than an acid.

Semaglutide also carries Aib in the second position of its chain. Its developers designed it to be fully stable against breakdown, and its EU label says it resists the enzyme DPP-4.<Cite id="lau-2015-semaglutide-discovery,ema-epar-wegovy" /> The tirzepatide labels do not state the purpose of its two Aib residues.<Cite id="fda-label-mounjaro,ema-epar-mounjaro" /> The formula is C225H348N48O68 and the molecular mass <Q>4813.53 Da</Q>.<Cite id="fda-label-mounjaro" /> Its development code was LY3298176.<Cite id="chembl-tirzepatide" />

### Where it came from

The gut releases two "incretin" hormones after a meal, GIP and GLP-1; both make the pancreas release more insulin.<Cite id="holst-2020-gip-coagonists" /> In 1993 a study of 9 people with type 2 diabetes and 9 matched controls found that <Claim level="human-limited">GIP's maximum insulin effect was 54% lower in diabetes, while GLP-1 kept most of its effect</Claim>.<Cite id="nauck-1993-gip-type2" /> Lilly's chemists later built GLP-1 activity into a GIP-based chain, citing evidence that the response to GIP largely returns once glucose is brought down. The result is a "dual agonist": one molecule that switches on two receptors.<Cite id="coskun-2018-ly3298176,willard-2020-imbalanced-biased" />

### Pens, vials and why "tirzepatide" is not one thing

In the EU, Mounjaro comes as single-dose pens, single-dose vials of ready-made solution, and multi-dose KwikPens.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" /> The evidence on this page belongs to these products: a known substance and amount, sterile, with an updated label. A powder in a vial sold online as "research tirzepatide" has none of these guarantees; the UK regulator states that GLP-1 powders in vials are not authorised.<Cite id="mhra-glp1-what-you-need-to-know" />

Tirzepatide switches on two gut-hormone receptors for days at a time. Most of its effects, wanted and unwanted, follow from where those receptors are found.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" />

### What each step rests on

**Staying in the blood.** The EU label gives an elimination half-life of about five days, 99% albumin binding and 80% absorption from the injection site, measured in people.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" /> The chain is mechanical: the fatty-acid tail binds albumin, and the EU label says this prolongs the half-life. Albumin is too large for the kidneys to filter, and tirzepatide is removed mainly by being broken down. So the drug is cleared slowly, and side effects do not end the day it is stopped.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro,coskun-2018-ly3298176" />

**Two receptors, unevenly.** <Claim level="in-vitro">In cell assays, tirzepatide bound the GIP receptor as strongly as natural GIP, and the GLP-1 receptor about five times more weakly than natural GLP-1</Claim>.<Cite id="coskun-2018-ly3298176,ema-epar-mounjaro" /> <Claim level="in-vitro">At the GLP-1 receptor it favoured cAMP signalling over recruiting β-arrestin, and caused less receptor internalisation than GLP-1</Claim>. <Claim level="animal">Experiments in isolated mouse islets suggest that this bias may help insulin release</Claim>. The authors, mostly company scientists, propose their receptor-occupancy estimates mainly for generating hypotheses.<Cite id="willard-2020-imbalanced-biased" />

**Insulin, glucagon and sensitivity in people.** <Claim level="human-controlled">In a 28-week, double-blind, randomised trial in 117 people with type 2 diabetes, tirzepatide improved insulin secretion and insulin sensitivity more than placebo and more than semaglutide at <Q>1 mg</Q>, and lowered glucagon after meals</Claim>.<Cite id="heise-2022-islet-function" /> Because cAMP amplifies an insulin release that glucose has already started, tirzepatide on its own rarely causes low blood sugar (see the risks section).<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" />

**Appetite and the stomach.** <Claim level="human-controlled">In the same trial, tirzepatide and semaglutide reduced appetite and energy intake at a test lunch by similar amounts, yet tirzepatide reduced weight and fat mass more</Claim>; the lunch did not explain the difference.<Cite id="heise-2023-appetite" /> The EU label says the delay in stomach emptying diminishes over time.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" />

### The open question: what does the GIP half do?

Here the evidence is weakest. The results point in two directions, and most of them come from animals:

- **GIP agonism helps.** <Claim level="animal">In obese mice without GLP-1 receptors, tirzepatide still improved insulin sensitivity, through more glucose uptake into fat tissue</Claim>.<Cite id="samms-2021-gipr-insulin-sensitivity" /> <Claim level="animal">In mice, a GIP agonist lowered food intake through GIP receptors in the brain, and the extra weight loss of a GIP/GLP-1 co-agonist disappeared in mice lacking those brain receptors</Claim>.<Cite id="zhang-2021-cns-gipr" /> <Claim level="animal">In mice, rats and musk shrews, GIP receptor activation reduced nausea and vomiting caused by a GLP-1 drug</Claim>.<Cite id="borner-2021-gipr-emesis" />
- **GIP blocking also helps.** <Claim level="animal">Antibodies that block the GIP receptor protected obese mice against weight gain and caused weight loss in obese monkeys</Claim>.<Cite id="killion-2018-gipr-antagonist" /> <Claim level="animal">Long exposure to a GIP agonist switched off GIP receptor activity in mouse fat cells, so the agonist ended up acting like a blocker</Claim>.<Cite id="killion-2020-gipr-desensitisation" /> In people, a different medicine that combines a GLP-1 agonist with a GIP-blocking antibody was tested in a phase 2 trial in 465 adults with obesity: <Claim level="human-controlled">mean weight fell 12.3% to 16.2% over 52 weeks, versus 2.5% with placebo</Claim>.<Cite id="jastreboff-2025-maridebart" />

<Callout type="uncertain" title="Agonist or functional blocker?">
That both switching on and blocking the GIP receptor lower weight is a puzzle. One proposed explanation is that a long-acting agonist eventually desensitises the receptor, so the net effect resembles blocking ("functional antagonism").<Cite id="holst-2020-gip-coagonists,killion-2020-gipr-desensitisation" /> <Claim level="hypothesis">Whether tirzepatide works in people partly as a functional GIP blocker, as a true GIP activator, or mainly through its GLP-1 action is not settled</Claim>. We found no study in people that separates the two receptors' contributions; the first human study of tirzepatide says its design could not do so.<Cite id="coskun-2018-ly3298176" />
</Callout>

<Section id="evidence">

<StudyTable />

Below is what the key studies tested, in whom, and what they could not show. The cell and animal studies explain the mechanism; they do not show that the medicine works in people.

### Type 2 diabetes

<Claim level="established">Tirzepatide lowers HbA1c in type 2 diabetes</Claim>. In SURPASS-1, a double-blind phase 3 trial in 478 adults on diet and exercise alone, HbA1c fell by 1.87 to 2.07 percentage points over 40 weeks versus a rise of 0.04 with placebo, without clinically significant low blood sugar.<Cite id="rosenstock-2021-surpass-1" /> In the earlier phase 2 trial (318 people), stomach and gut side effects rose with dose, from 23.1% to 66.0%.<Cite id="frias-2018-tirzepatide-phase2" />

SURPASS-2 compared tirzepatide with semaglutide <Q>1 mg</Q> in 1,879 adults on metformin over 40 weeks. <Claim level="human-controlled">HbA1c fell by 2.01 to 2.30 percentage points with tirzepatide and 1.86 with semaglutide; weight differences were 1.9 to 5.5 kg</Claim>.<Cite id="frias-2021-surpass-2" /> The trial was open-label, and semaglutide was used at a lower dose than those later authorised. Against insulin glargine (SURPASS-4, 2,002 adults at high cardiovascular risk), HbA1c fell more and low blood sugar was less frequent (6–9% versus 19%).<Cite id="delprato-2021-surpass-4" /> In 99 young people aged 10 to 17 (SURPASS-PEDS), HbA1c fell by 2.23 percentage points over 30 weeks.<Cite id="hannon-2025-surpass-peds" />

### The heart: SURPASS-CVOT

SURPASS-CVOT randomised 13,299 people with type 2 diabetes and atherosclerotic disease to tirzepatide or dulaglutide, an older GLP-1 medicine that had itself reduced cardiovascular events in an earlier trial. Over a median of about four years, <Claim level="human-controlled">heart attack, stroke or cardiovascular death occurred in 12.2% with tirzepatide and 13.1% with dulaglutide (HR 0.92, 95.3% CI 0.83–1.01)</Claim>. That met the test for "not worse"; it did not show "better".<Cite id="nicholls-2025-surpass-cvot" /> All-cause death was 8.5% versus 10.1% in the US label, a result not controlled for multiple testing.<Cite id="fda-label-mounjaro" />

The regulators went further than the trial's own conclusion. The EMA accepted that Mounjaro reduces serious cardiovascular events in this group, and the FDA added an indication to reduce this risk.<Cite id="ema-2026-mounjaro-cvot-outcome,fda-drugsatfda-mounjaro" /> That conclusion leans on dulaglutide's earlier benefit. Without a placebo group, the size of the benefit against no treatment is an inference, not a measurement.

### Weight management, and what happens after stopping

SURMOUNT-1 randomised 2,539 adults with obesity or overweight, without diabetes. <Claim level="established">Mean weight fell 15.0%, 19.5% and 20.9% at the three doses versus 3.1% with placebo over 72 weeks</Claim>; 4.3% to 7.1% stopped because of side effects, versus 2.6% on placebo.<Cite id="jastreboff-2022-surmount-1" /> Among the 1,032 participants with prediabetes, treated for 176 weeks, type 2 diabetes was diagnosed in 1.3% versus 13.3%.<Cite id="jastreboff-2025-surmount-1-prediabetes" /> Weight loss itself lowers diabetes risk, so this does not show an effect beyond weight.

<Claim level="human-controlled">Much of the weight returns when treatment stops</Claim>. In SURMOUNT-4, 670 adults who had lost a mean 20.9% over 36 weeks were randomised: those who continued lost a further 5.5%, those switched to placebo regained 14.0% over 52 weeks.<Cite id="aronne-2024-surmount-4" /> In SURMOUNT-MAINTAIN, 378 adults were randomised after 60 weeks to continue, to a lower dose (<Q>5 mg</Q>) or to placebo; at week 112 their weight was 21.9%, 16.6% and 9.9% below the starting weight.<Cite id="horn-2026-surmount-maintain" />

### Head to head with semaglutide

SURMOUNT-5 randomised 751 adults with obesity but no diabetes to the highest tolerated dose of tirzepatide or of semaglutide for 72 weeks. Mean weight fell 20.2% with tirzepatide and 13.7% with semaglutide; waist size fell 18.4 cm and 13.0 cm.<Cite id="aronne-2025-surmount-5" /> The trial was open-label and run by the maker of one of the two drugs. An independent US health-record study of 18,386 matched adults pointed the same way (6.9 percentage points more weight loss at 12 months), but cannot rule out confounding.<Cite id="rodriguez-2024-tirzepatide-semaglutide-cohort" /> Neither shows which drug suits a given person.

### Sleep apnoea, heart failure and the liver

In two SURMOUNT-OSA trials (469 adults with obesity and moderate to severe sleep apnoea), <Claim level="human-controlled">breathing interruptions per hour fell by 20.0 and 23.8 events more than with placebo over 52 weeks</Claim>.<Cite id="malhotra-2024-surmount-osa,ema-2024-mounjaro-osa-outcome" /> In SUMMIT, 731 adults with obesity and heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF), <Claim level="human-controlled">cardiovascular death or worsening heart failure occurred in 9.9% versus 15.3% (HR 0.62)</Claim>, driven by heart-failure events; cardiovascular deaths were few and not lower (8 versus 5 in the paper; the EU label gives 10 versus 5).<Cite id="packer-2025-summit,ema-epar-mounjaro" /> The EMA was not sure this is more than an effect of weight loss.<Cite id="ema-2026-mounjaro-hfpef-outcome" /> In a phase 2 trial in 190 people with the liver disease MASH and moderate or severe scarring, <Claim level="human-controlled">MASH resolved without worse scarring in 44% to 62% with tirzepatide versus 10% with placebo at 52 weeks</Claim>; the authors call for larger and longer trials.<Cite id="loomba-2024-synergy-nash" />

### Who paid for the evidence

Every pivotal trial here was funded by Eli Lilly, with many company authors. A crude PubMed count on 20 October 2026 found 134 randomised-trial records on tirzepatide, of which only 13 had no Lilly-affiliated author.<Cite id="pubmed-tirzepatide-rct-count-2026-10-20" /> That does not make the results false, but the questions, comparators and doses were mostly the manufacturer's choice. Independent evidence comes mainly from health-record studies and other groups' laboratory work.<Cite id="rodriguez-2024-tirzepatide-semaglutide-cohort,killion-2018-gipr-antagonist,zhang-2021-cns-gipr" />

</Section>

<Section id="unknowns">

- **Use beyond about four years.** The longest trial, SURPASS-CVOT, followed people for a median of about four years.<Cite id="nicholls-2025-surpass-cvot" />
- **How to stop.** In the trials, switching to placebo led to substantial weight regain. A lower dose kept part of the loss in one trial. No trial has yet shown a way to stop entirely and keep the benefit.<Cite id="aronne-2024-surmount-4,horn-2026-surmount-maintain" />
- **What GIP adds in people.** Animal studies point both ways; we found no human study that separates the two receptors.<Cite id="killion-2020-gipr-desensitisation,samms-2021-gipr-insulin-sensitivity" />
- **Thyroid tumours.** Tirzepatide caused thyroid C-cell tumours in rats; the EU label calls the human relevance unknown.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" />
- **Muscle and function.** The EU label reports that fat mass fell more than lean mass in a body-composition substudy; whether lean-mass loss matters for strength and falls in older people is open.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro,neeland-2024-lean-mass" />
- **Pregnancy.** Animal studies showed foetal harm at doses toxic to the mother; human data are very limited.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" />
- **Anti-drug antibodies.** Between about half and two-thirds of treated people develop them across the trials. No effect on efficacy was found, but allergic and injection-site reactions were more frequent in people who had them. What this means over many years is not known.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro,fda-label-mounjaro" />

</Section>

### European Union

Mounjaro is authorised through the centralised procedure, valid in every member state; the marketing authorisation holder is Eli Lilly Nederland B.V.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" /> The EMA assessed three further uses and in each case added the trial data to the label without a separate indication, because the use was already covered: sleep apnoea (December 2024), HFpEF (January 2026) and cardiovascular risk reduction (June 2026).<Cite id="ema-2024-mounjaro-osa-outcome,ema-2026-mounjaro-hfpef-outcome,ema-2026-mounjaro-cvot-outcome" /> Mounjaro is under additional monitoring, so that new safety information is picked up quickly.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" />

### Netherlands

The EU authorisation applies directly; tirzepatide is prescription-only. Reimbursement is narrow:

- **Type 2 diabetes:** reimbursed only under the conditions of Bijlage 2 of the Regeling zorgverzekering, for adults. One route is a BMI of 30 or more when metformin plus a sulfonylurea, or metformin plus basal insulin, is not enough. The other is very high cardiovascular risk, added to an SGLT2 inhibitor and metformin.<Cite id="fk-regeling-zorgverzekering-bijlage-2-tirzepatide" />
- **Weight management:** not reimbursed. The Zorginstituut is assessing it in steps, first for people with a BMI of 30 or more with related disease, or a BMI of 35 or more. The first scientific discussion was on 5 October 2026; consultation is expected in early November 2026.<Cite id="zin-tirzepatide-obesity-assessment" />

Dutch GP guidelines, as summarised in the Farmacotherapeutisch Kompas, advise against tirzepatide for type 2 diabetes without very high cardiovascular risk, because there is little experience with it and the costs are very high. The GP guideline on obesity also advises against it in primary care. In hospital care, the same summary says obesity medicines can be considered earlier, but it names liraglutide, naltrexone/bupropion and semaglutide, not tirzepatide.<Cite id="fk-tirzepatide" /> This gap has costs and reasons. An authorised weight medicine with strong trial results exists, but people who cannot pay do not get it, while the CBG warns against buying these medicines online without a prescription.<Cite id="cbg-glp1-position" /> Equally, the costs are real. When it assessed semaglutide (Wegovy) in 2024, the Zorginstituut estimated that 4.1 million people in the Netherlands met the EU weight criteria, which are the same as Mounjaro's. Costs could in theory reach €10 billion a year; its practical estimate was €1.3 billion a year. That is why it now assesses new obesity medicines first for the people with the most health problems, still an estimated group of more than 1 million.<Cite id="zin-2025-obesity-staged-approach,zin-tirzepatide-obesity-assessment" />

### Belgium

Authorised through the EU procedure; Belgian law requires a marketing authorisation, national or European, before a medicine is placed on the market.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro,be-medicines-law-1964" /> We could not verify Belgian reimbursement rules on an official source on 20 October 2026.

<Section id="risks">

Unless stated otherwise, risks and frequencies come from the EU product information.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" />

### Stomach and gut

In the placebo-controlled weight trial in people without diabetes, stomach and gut problems occurred in 55.6% to 60.8% with tirzepatide versus 30.3% with placebo; nausea in 24.6% to 33.3% versus 9.5%. Most were mild or moderate and came while the dose was being raised; 1.9% to 4.4% stopped permanently because of them.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" /> Two mechanisms are proposed: the stomach empties more slowly, and <Claim level="animal">in animals, GLP-1 drugs act on hindbrain areas that also process nausea signals</Claim>.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro,borner-2021-gipr-emesis" /> Vomiting and diarrhoea can cause dehydration, which can lead to acute kidney failure; older people are more at risk.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" /> The US label also lists reports after approval of ileus (the bowel stops moving), blockage of the bowel and severe constipation.<Cite id="fda-label-mounjaro" />

### Gallbladder and pancreas

Gallstones and inflammation of the gallbladder are uncommon side effects (up to 1 in 100). Inflammation of the gallbladder occurred in 0.6% versus 0.2% in the pooled weight trials; in the HFpEF trial, acute gallbladder disease occurred in 4.4% versus 2.7%. In the weight trials, the label notes, acute gallbladder events were more common in people who lost more weight.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" /> Across 76 trials of GLP-1 medicines (103,371 people), gallbladder and bile-duct disease was more frequent, more so in weight-loss trials, at higher doses and with longer use.<Cite id="he-2022-gallbladder" />

Acute pancreatitis is an uncommon side effect (up to 1 in 100). Tirzepatide should be stopped if pancreatitis is suspected and not restarted if it is confirmed.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" /> The US label lists reports after approval of haemorrhagic and necrotising pancreatitis, sometimes fatal.<Cite id="fda-label-mounjaro" /> In the trials, the pancreatic enzymes amylase and lipase rose by about 23% to 42% on average. Raised enzymes alone, without symptoms, do not predict pancreatitis. The EU label does not propose a mechanism for the pancreatitis cases.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" />

### Low blood sugar

Tirzepatide's insulin effect depends on glucose, but insulin and sulfonylureas act regardless of glucose. In the diabetes trials, clinically significant low blood sugar occurred in 10–14% when tirzepatide was added to a sulfonylurea and 14–19% when added to basal insulin. The label says the risk can be lowered by reducing the dose of those medicines.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" />

### Thyroid C-cell tumours in rats

In a two-year study, tirzepatide caused thyroid C-cell tumours in rats at all doses tested, at exposures up to about the human one; a six-month study in a mouse strain found none. The EU label calls the human relevance unknown, and lists raised blood calcitonin as common in some trial groups.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro,fda-label-mounjaro" /> <Claim level="animal">Rodent C-cells carry GLP-1 receptors, and GLP-1 agonists stimulated calcitonin release and C-cell growth in rats; human and monkey C-cells had few receptors</Claim>. That study tested liraglutide, not tirzepatide.<Cite id="knudsen-2010-thyroid-c-cells" />

### Anaesthesia, the contraceptive pill and other tablets

Slower stomach emptying means food may still be in the stomach during general anaesthesia or deep sedation, and could be breathed into the lungs. Tell the doctor about tirzepatide before any procedure.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro,ema-prac-2024-07-aspiration" />

The same slowing reduces the absorption of some tablets, most at the start of treatment. A single dose lowered peak levels of a combined contraceptive pill by 55% to 66%. Here the regulators differ. The EU label considers this not clinically relevant and requires no change.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" /> The US labels and the UK regulator advise switching to a non-oral method, or adding a barrier method such as condoms, for four weeks after starting and after each dose increase.<Cite id="fda-label-mounjaro,mhra-glp1-what-you-need-to-know" /> For tablets where small changes matter, such as warfarin or digoxin, the label advises monitoring.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" />

### Muscle and eyes

Weight loss on tirzepatide is mostly fat: in a body-composition substudy, fat mass fell more than lean mass.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" /> <Claim level="human-limited">Across GLP-1-based medicines, lean mass has made up from about 15% to 40–60% of the weight lost</Claim>; lean mass also includes organs and water, and the source is a narrative review.<Cite id="neeland-2024-lean-mass" /> In people with diabetic retinopathy, a fast fall in glucose can temporarily worsen it; the label asks for close monitoring.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" />

### Mood, pregnancy and other effects

Mounjaro was not part of the EMA's 2024 review of suicidal thoughts with GLP-1 medicines, which found no causal link for the medicines it reviewed.<Cite id="ema-prac-2024-04-suicidal-thoughts" /> The UK regulator, after its own safety review of GLP-1 medicines, concluded that the data do not support a causal link with depression or suicidal thoughts.<Cite id="mhra-glp1-what-you-need-to-know" /> If your mood changes on any medicine, tell your doctor.

In rats tirzepatide caused foetal malformations and growth reduction at doses toxic to the mother. The label does not recommend it in pregnancy, recommends contraception, and says to stop at least a month before a planned pregnancy.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" />

The label also lists a rise in heart rate (average increases of 2 to 6 beats per minute across trials), hair loss, dizziness, low blood pressure, tiredness, injection-site reactions, and rare allergic reactions including anaphylaxis and angioedema.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" /> Reports of lithium poisoning have been made when GIP/GLP-1 medicines were started or increased in people taking lithium.<Cite id="fk-tirzepatide" />

<Section id="harm-reduction">

For an authorised medicine, the legal route has the fewest unknowns. This section gives no doses: your prescriber decides the dose.

### The legal route in the Netherlands

Tirzepatide needs a prescription from a doctor and comes from a pharmacy. For type 2 diabetes it may be reimbursed under strict conditions. For weight management, as of 20 October 2026, basic health insurance does not pay.<Cite id="fk-regeling-zorgverzekering-bijlage-2-tirzepatide,zin-tirzepatide-obesity-assessment" /> Dutch guidelines put lifestyle treatment first, such as a combined lifestyle programme (GLI).<Cite id="fk-tirzepatide" /> A prescriber can check whether the medicine suits you. They can look for interactions with your other medicines and follow you up. Without a prescriber, none of that happens.<Cite id="belcourt-2025-research-purposes" /> See [talking to your doctor](/en/talking-to-your-doctor/).

### Why an online "research" vial is a different product

The FDA has warned companies that sold tirzepatide falsely labelled "for research purposes" or "not for human consumption". It says these products are of unknown quality and may be harmful.<Cite id="fda-unapproved-glp1-concerns" /> The CBG's summary: you do not know what is in it, how to use it, or what it will do to your health.<Cite id="cbg-glp1-position" /> See [product risk](/en/safety/product-risk/).

By 31 May 2026 the FDA had received more than 730 adverse-event reports for compounded tirzepatide in the US. It says such reports are likely to be incomplete. It also reported products with fake pharmacy labels. Some reports came from people prescribed doses higher, more frequent or raised faster than the approved label allows.<Cite id="fda-unapproved-glp1-concerns" /> In US reports, compounded GLP-1 products were linked more often with preparation errors and contamination than branded ones.<Cite id="mccall-2026-compounded-faers" /> Multi-dose vials add risk: the FDA advises a new sterile needle and syringe every time, and discarding a vial 28 days after first use.<Cite id="fda-unapproved-glp1-concerns" /> See [sterile technique](/en/safety/sterile-technique/).

### Fake pens

In the UK, falsified Mounjaro KwikPens labelled <Q>15 mg</Q> carried the batch number of a genuine <Q>7.5 mg</Q> pen and had faulty dose knobs. Their sterility could not be assured, so infection or allergic reactions were possible.<Cite id="mhra-2026-falsified-mounjaro" /> In October 2026 the UK regulator seized more than 12,000 fake retatrutide and tirzepatide pens.<Cite id="mhra-2026-10-counterfeit-pens" />

### Who should not use it, and interactions

- **Pregnancy, trying to conceive:** animal studies showed harm to the unborn baby. Stop at least one month before a planned pregnancy.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" />
- **The contraceptive pill:** absorption may drop at the start and after each dose increase. Ask your prescriber about extra protection. The UK and US regulators advise adding a barrier method, such as condoms, for four weeks each time, or switching to a method that is not a pill.<Cite id="mhra-glp1-what-you-need-to-know,fda-label-mounjaro" />
- **Personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma, or MEN 2:** contraindicated in the US because of the rat finding; not a formal EU contraindication, so discuss it.<Cite id="fda-label-mounjaro,ema-epar-mounjaro" />
- **Previous pancreatitis or severe gastroparesis:** not studied; use with caution, because tirzepatide is linked to pancreatitis and slows the stomach.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" />
- **Insulin or sulfonylureas (such as gliclazide):** higher risk of low blood sugar.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" /> **Other GLP-1 medicines:** the US label says that using them together with tirzepatide is not recommended.<Cite id="fda-label-zepbound" />
- **Warfarin, digoxin, lithium:** absorption or blood levels can change, mostly at the start and after a dose increase. Extra checks (such as INR or drug levels) are advised.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro,fk-tirzepatide" />

### Checks that make sense, and why

- **Blood glucose**, if you also use insulin or a sulfonylurea, because the combination can push sugar too low.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" />
- **Kidney function** after prolonged vomiting or diarrhoea, because dehydration can harm the kidneys.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" />
- **Eyes**, if you have diabetic retinopathy, because a fast fall in glucose can worsen it for a while.<Cite id="ema-epar-mounjaro" />

### When to stop and get help

<Callout type="stop" title="Get medical help now">
- **Severe, constant pain in the upper belly**, often spreading to the back, with or without vomiting: possible pancreatitis. Stop tirzepatide and get urgent care.
- **Vomiting so much you cannot keep fluids down, or passing very little urine:** risk of dehydration and kidney injury. Get same-day care.
- **Shaking, sweating, confusion or drowsiness**, especially if you also use insulin or a sulfonylurea: possible low blood sugar. Take something sugary if you can swallow safely; call 112 if the person becomes drowsy or confused.
- **Swelling of the face, lips or throat, or difficulty breathing:** possible allergic reaction. Call 112.
- **Spreading redness, heat or pus at the injection site, or fever, after using a product from outside a pharmacy:** possible infection. Get urgent care.
</Callout>
