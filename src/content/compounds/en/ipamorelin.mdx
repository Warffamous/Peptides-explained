<Section id="what-it-is">

<Lead question="What is ipamorelin?">A lab-made peptide that makes the body release growth hormone. It is not an approved medicine.</Lead>

US wellness clinics and websites promote ipamorelin for weight loss, muscle, sleep and anti-ageing, often mixed with CJC-1295 or sermorelin. The FDA looked for evidence on growth hormone deficiency and on bowel recovery after surgery. It found one bowel-surgery trial, and that trial was negative.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />

<InDetail title="The molecule, and how it is made">

Ipamorelin is a pentapeptide: <Seq>Aib-His-D-2Nal-D-Phe-Lys-NH2</Seq>. Its formula is C38H49N9O5 and its mass <Q>711.9 g/mol</Q>.<Cite id="pubchem-ipamorelin,raun-1998-ipamorelin" /> Only two of its five building blocks, histidine and lysine, are ordinary protein amino acids:<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing,chembl-ipamorelin" />

- **Aib** (2-aminoisobutyric acid) at the start, the same unusual amino acid used in semaglutide.<Cite id="pubchem-semaglutide" />
- **D-2Nal**, a mirror-image form of naphthylalanine, an amino acid with a large double ring.
- **D-Phe**, the mirror-image form of phenylalanine.
- An **amide** at the end instead of the usual acid group.

<Claim level="hypothesis">The enzymes that cut peptides are built for the usual building blocks, so mirror-image amino acids and Aib probably slow its breakdown.</Claim> In rats, most of an injected dose left the body unchanged in urine and bile, which fits that idea.<Cite id="johansen-1998-ipamorelin-pk" />

### How it is made

It is made by solid-phase peptide synthesis: amino acids are added one at a time to a chain anchored on resin beads, then the chain is cut free and purified.<Cite id="raun-1998-ipamorelin,fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" /> Each step can leave chains that are too short or slightly wrong, and leftover solvents and reagents. The FDA notes that unusual amino acids make such impurities harder to measure.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />

The free base barely dissolves in water; a university laboratory had to add acetic acid to dissolve it.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing,greenwood-2012-ipamorelin-gastric" /> The acetate salt dissolves. Both forms are sold, and the FDA treats them as different substances.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Where it came from, and why testing stopped">

Researchers at Novo Nordisk in Denmark made ipamorelin in the mid-1990s, under the code NNC 26-0161.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing,johansen-1998-ipamorelin-pk" /> In 1998 they called it "the first selective growth hormone secretagogue": a substance that makes the pituitary gland release growth hormone without, in their pig experiments, also releasing the stress hormones ACTH and cortisol.<Cite id="raun-1998-ipamorelin,pubchem-ipamorelin" /> It has an official international non-proprietary name, from the WHO's family of "-morelin" growth hormone-releasing peptides.<Cite id="who-2014-inn-biological-review" />

The Swiss company Helsinn later developed it as a drip to restart the bowel after surgery.<Cite id="greenwood-2012-ipamorelin-gastric,ctgov-nct00672074" /> It ran two phase 2 trials in the United States between 2008 and 2014. The first showed no significant benefit; the second, with 320 patients, was never published.<Cite id="ctgov-nct00672074,ctgov-nct01280344,beck-2014-ipamorelin-ileus" /> The FDA, quoting a 2020 review, says development was reportedly stopped because of the disappointing results.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />

### Related medicines that did reach patients

Ipamorelin belongs to a real drug class. Macimorelin, another substance that triggers growth hormone release, is authorised in the EU (as Ghryvelin, since 11 January 2019) as a single-dose test for growth hormone deficiency in adults.<Cite id="ema-epar-ghryvelin" /> The EU refused anamorelin, a ghrelin-receptor tablet meant to treat weight loss and muscle wasting in people with lung cancer, in 2017: its effect on lean body mass was marginal, and no effect on grip strength or quality of life was shown.<Cite id="ema-epar-adlumiz" /> So these drugs can move hormones in people. A lasting benefit from long-term use has not been shown.

### How it is used now

In the United States, outsourcing facilities reported making ipamorelin injections from at least 2017 until 2020. Compounding pharmacy websites offer it mostly as injections or as lozenges that melt in the mouth, often mixed with CJC-1295 or sermorelin. One adverse-event report to the FDA involved a nasal spray.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />

</InDetail>

<Section id="mechanism">

<Lead question="How does it work?">It copies a hunger hormone. A gland under the brain then sends out a burst of growth hormone.</Lead>

<Diagram id="ipamorelin-gh-axis" caption="Where ipamorelin acts in the growth hormone system, and how well each step is shown.">

| Step | What happens | Shown in |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The brain sets the rhythm | The hypothalamus sends GHRH (a "go" signal) and somatostatin (a "stop" signal) to the pituitary gland, so growth hormone comes in pulses. | People (normal body function) |
| 2. Ghrelin or ipamorelin | Ghrelin from the stomach, or ipamorelin, switches on the ghrelin receptor on pituitary cells and in the hypothalamus. | Cells and animals |
| 3. A growth hormone pulse | The pituitary releases a burst of growth hormone. After ipamorelin into a vein: one peak at about 40 minutes, very low again by 6 hours. | People (healthy men, one drip) |
| 4. IGF-1 from the liver | Growth hormone makes the liver release IGF-1, which carries many of its growth effects. | Rats, with mixed results; not reported in people given ipamorelin |
| 5. The brake | Growth hormone and IGF-1 switch on somatostatin and damp the next pulse (negative feedback). | People (normal body function) |
| 6. Other places with the same receptor | The stomach, gut nerves, pancreas and brain areas for hunger and reward. | Animals; ghrelin itself in people |

</Diagram>

<Cite id="veldhuis-2001-gh-pulsatility,desousa-2025-gh-feedback,howard-1996-ghs-receptor,raun-1998-ipamorelin,gobburu-1999-ipamorelin-pkpd,medlineplus-igf1,johansen-1999-ipamorelin-bone,malmlof-1999-ipamorelin-igf1,muller-2015-ghrelin-review" />

Ipamorelin contains no growth hormone. It works through the body's own pituitary gland, so it can only release what that gland can make. The FDA notes that it would likely not help people whose pituitary gland makes no growth hormone at all.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />

<InDetail title="Step by step: the chain and what each step rests on">

<Mechanism />

### The receptor and the hormone it copies

In 1996 Merck scientists found the receptor that synthetic growth hormone-releasing substances act on, in the pituitary gland and hypothalamus of pigs and people. These substances "stimulate and amplify pulsatile growth hormone (GH) release".<Cite id="howard-1996-ghs-receptor" /> In 1999 a Japanese group found its natural partner in the rat stomach: ghrelin, a 28-amino-acid peptide.<Cite id="kojima-1999-ghrelin" /> Besides hunger, ghrelin is now known to act on gut movement, stomach acid, blood sugar, sleep and reward-seeking.<Cite id="muller-2015-ghrelin-review,wren-2001-ghrelin-appetite" />

<Claim level="in-vitro">In rat pituitary cells in a dish, ipamorelin released growth hormone with a potency and strength similar to GHRP-6; tests with blockers showed that it acts through the same receptor type, not through the GHRH receptor</Claim>.<Cite id="raun-1998-ipamorelin" /> The FDA describes the receptor's signal as running mainly through the Gq protein and phospholipase.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />

### The growth hormone pulse in people

Growth hormone normally comes in bursts, driven by GHRH and held back by somatostatin. Each burst switches on somatostatin, which damps the next one; IGF-1 adds a slower brake.<Cite id="veldhuis-2001-gh-pulsatility,desousa-2025-gh-feedback" /> <Claim level="human-controlled">In healthy men, one drip of ipamorelin into a vein produced one growth hormone pulse that peaked after about 40 minutes and had fallen to very low levels by 6 hours</Claim>; the peptide's half-life was about 2 hours.<Cite id="gobburu-1999-ipamorelin-pkpd,fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />

### IGF-1, bone and weight

The evidence after the pulse is from rats, and it is mixed. <Claim level="animal">Three times daily for 15 days, ipamorelin made rats' long bones grow faster and raised their weight, but total IGF-1 did not change</Claim>.<Cite id="johansen-1999-ipamorelin-bone" /> In another rat study, IGF-1 rose with repeated doses into a vein.<Cite id="malmlof-1999-ipamorelin-igf1" /> In the 15-day study, the growth hormone response to a test dose was slightly smaller afterwards, an early hint that the pituitary may answer less with repeated pushing.<Cite id="johansen-1999-ipamorelin-bone,fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" /> We found no published measurement of IGF-1 in people given ipamorelin.

### The gut

<Claim level="animal">After abdominal surgery in rats, ipamorelin into a vein brought the first bowel movement forward and emptied the stomach faster</Claim>; in stomach muscle in a dish it restored contractions, as ghrelin did.<Cite id="venkova-2009-ipamorelin-ileus,greenwood-2012-ipamorelin-gastric" /> <Claim level="human-controlled">In patients after bowel surgery, the effect on recovery was not statistically significant</Claim>.<Cite id="beck-2014-ipamorelin-ileus" />

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Why 'selective' in pigs does not mean 'safe' in people">

Ipamorelin is often described as "selective". That word comes from one 1998 experiment. <Claim level="animal">In awake pigs, GHRP-6 and GHRP-2 raised the stress hormones ACTH and cortisol, but ipamorelin did not, even at more than 200 times the dose needed for half its growth hormone effect</Claim>. None of the three changed the reproductive hormones LH and FSH, prolactin or thyroid-stimulating hormone in those pigs.<Cite id="raun-1998-ipamorelin" />

That result matters, because natural ghrelin is not selective in people. <Claim level="human-limited">In four healthy men, a ghrelin injection raised ACTH, cortisol and prolactin as well as growth hormone</Claim>.<Cite id="takaya-2000-ghrelin-humans" /> But four gaps separate the pig finding from safety in people:

1. **Species and dose.** It was shown in pigs, after single doses. The study in healthy men measured growth hormone, not cortisol or prolactin.<Cite id="gobburu-1999-ipamorelin-pkpd,fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />
2. **Only pituitary hormones.** "Selective" says nothing about the same receptor in the stomach, pancreas and brain. That receptor also drives hunger, gut movement and blood sugar.<Cite id="muller-2015-ghrelin-review" />
3. **More growth hormone has its own risks.** For approved growth hormone medicines, the labels warn about high blood sugar and diabetes, fluid retention, raised pressure in the skull and possible tumour growth. The FDA sees no data showing ipamorelin avoids these.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />
4. **The product may not be pure ipamorelin.** Any selectivity belongs to the exact molecule, not to whatever is in a vial.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing,gajda-2019-glycine-ghs" />

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Hunger and blood sugar: what the ghrelin receptor does">

Ghrelin is a hunger hormone. <Claim level="human-controlled">In a double-blind crossover study in nine volunteers, a ghrelin drip made people eat 28% more from a buffet</Claim>.<Cite id="wren-2001-ghrelin-appetite" /> <Claim level="animal">In mice, ipamorelin raised body fat, also in mice that make little growth hormone; growth hormone releasers, but not growth hormone itself, raised food intake</Claim>.<Cite id="lall-2001-ghs-adiposity" /> How much ipamorelin changes appetite in people has not been measured.

The blood-sugar picture is mixed. <Claim level="human-controlled">In 11 volunteers, ghrelin raised blood sugar and lowered insulin within minutes, while hexarelin, a synthetic growth hormone releaser, did not</Claim>.<Cite id="broglio-2001-ghrelin-glucose" /> <Claim level="in-vitro">In pieces of rat pancreas, ipamorelin increased insulin release</Claim>.<Cite id="adeghate-2004-ipamorelin-insulin" /> But in the bowel-surgery trial, high blood sugar when leaving hospital was more common with ipamorelin (14.3% versus 8.6%).<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" /> And over a year, <Claim level="human-controlled">a related daily tablet, MK-677, raised fasting blood sugar and lowered insulin sensitivity in 65 healthy older adults</Claim>.<Cite id="nass-2008-mk677-older-adults" />

</InDetail>

The picture is not empty. In healthy men, ipamorelin released growth hormone, and in rats it sped up the gut after surgery and made bones grow.<Cite id="gobburu-1999-ipamorelin-pkpd,greenwood-2012-ipamorelin-gastric,johansen-1999-ipamorelin-bone" /> But no study in people has tested the uses it is promoted for, and the only published patient trial missed its goal.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing,beck-2014-ipamorelin-ileus" />

<InDetail title="Cell and animal studies, one by one">

### The discovery work at Novo Nordisk

The first paper set out the basic profile. <Claim level="animal">In rat pituitary cells, anaesthetised rats and awake pigs, ipamorelin released growth hormone about as strongly as GHRP-6, without raising ACTH or cortisol in pigs</Claim>.<Cite id="raun-1998-ipamorelin" /> <Claim level="animal">In rats it was cleared from the blood five times more slowly than GHRP-6 and left the body mostly unchanged in urine</Claim>.<Cite id="johansen-1998-ipamorelin-pk" />

The same company and its university partners then tested longer use in rats. <Claim level="animal">Over 15 days, ipamorelin made adult female rats' long bones grow faster, by up to about a quarter, and increased weight gain</Claim>.<Cite id="johansen-1999-ipamorelin-bone" /> Over 12 weeks, a Swedish and British team with a Novo Nordisk co-author found that <Claim level="animal">ipamorelin, GHRP-6 and growth hormone all raised bone mineral, but only because bones grew bigger; per gram of body weight nothing changed</Claim>.<Cite id="svensson-2000-ipamorelin-bone" /> In rats given a steroid that weakens muscle and bone, <Claim level="animal">adding ipamorelin for 3 months kept calf muscles stronger and quadrupled the rate of new bone formation on the bone surface</Claim>.<Cite id="andersen-2001-ipamorelin-glucocorticoid" />

### Other groups

Independent academic studies are few and single. A Cambridge and Göteborg team found that <Claim level="animal">ipamorelin increased body fat in mice, also in mice that make little growth hormone, and that growth hormone releasers raised food intake</Claim>, the opposite of the fat-loss claims made for it today.<Cite id="lall-2001-ghs-adiposity" /> A group in the United Arab Emirates found that <Claim level="in-vitro">ipamorelin increased insulin release from pieces of rat pancreas</Claim>.<Cite id="adeghate-2004-ipamorelin-insulin" />

### The gut programme

A University of Oklahoma laboratory tested ipamorelin for the bowel paralysis that follows surgery. <Claim level="animal">One dose into a vein brought the first bowel movement forward in rats; repeated doses over 2 days increased stool output, food intake and weight</Claim>.<Cite id="venkova-2009-ipamorelin-ileus" /> In a later study, with ipamorelin supplied by Helsinn and a Helsinn co-author, <Claim level="animal">78% of a test meal stayed in the stomach of operated rats with a dummy drip, against 52% with ipamorelin</Claim>.<Cite id="greenwood-2012-ipamorelin-gastric" /> These are the results that led to the patient trials.

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Studies in people, and trials that were never published">

### Healthy volunteers

In a randomised, placebo-controlled phase 1 study, healthy men received one 15-minute drip into a vein: eight at each of five dose levels, six on ipamorelin and two on placebo.<Cite id="gobburu-1999-ipamorelin-pkpd,fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" /> That makes 40 men, although the FDA's summary gives 48 enrolled; the full paper may explain the difference.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" /> <Claim level="human-controlled">Ipamorelin had a half-life of about 2 hours; growth hormone rose in a single pulse that peaked after about 40 minutes and fell to very low levels by 6 hours</Claim>.<Cite id="gobburu-1999-ipamorelin-pkpd,fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" /> The abstract says growth hormone rose at all dose levels. The FDA's summary says the lowest-dose group had too little growth hormone to analyse.<Cite id="gobburu-1999-ipamorelin-pkpd,fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" /> The FDA notes that the paper does not mention any adverse events. It was built to measure drug levels, not safety.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />

### The bowel-surgery trial

Helsinn's phase 2 trial enrolled 117 adults having part of the bowel removed; 114 were treated. They received ipamorelin or placebo as a drip into a vein twice a day, from the day after surgery for up to 7 days.<Cite id="beck-2014-ipamorelin-ileus" /> <Claim level="human-controlled">The median time until patients kept down a first solid meal was 25.3 hours with ipamorelin and 32.6 hours with placebo (p = 0.15); time to first bowel movement, recovery of bowel function and hospital stay did not differ significantly either</Claim>.<Cite id="beck-2014-ipamorelin-ileus,fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" /> In a subgroup having open surgery, bowel recovery seemed faster with ipamorelin. The authors urged caution: the trial was small, with a wide mix of patients.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing,beck-2014-ipamorelin-ileus" />

### The trial nobody can read

Helsinn then ran a larger phase 2 trial in 320 patients, testing three dosing schedules, given into a vein, against placebo; it finished in May 2014. No results have been posted on the registry or published in a journal that we could find.<Cite id="ctgov-nct01280344" /> Whatever it showed, its silence means the published record is incomplete.

### Other human data

One volunteer took ipamorelin through the nose in a doping-test study: <Claim level="human-limited">the peptide was heavily broken down, and one breakdown product was still found in urine after the intact peptide had gone</Claim>.<Cite id="semenistaya-2015-ghrp-nasal" /> A US observational study of veterans lists ipamorelin among several supplements in a hormone programme given before other treatments; it cannot separate ipamorelin's effect.<Cite id="ctgov-nct07717866" /> As of 22 October 2026, ClinicalTrials.gov lists no running or planned trial of ipamorelin.<Cite id="ctgov-ipamorelin-search-2026-10-22" />

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Where the evidence is promising, and what the gap costs">

Some of the biology is solid. Ipamorelin releases growth hormone in people, and drugs that act on the same system have passed regulators: macimorelin as a diagnostic test.<Cite id="gobburu-1999-ipamorelin-pkpd,ema-epar-ghryvelin" /> In a 2-year trial in 65 older adults, the related tablet MK-677 raised growth hormone and IGF-1 to young-adult levels and added about 1 kg of fat-free mass. But strength and function did not change, although the authors say the trial was too small to judge them.<Cite id="nass-2008-mk677-older-adults" /> Raising a hormone level is not the same as making people stronger or healthier.

The gap has a cost. Bowel paralysis after surgery is a real problem with few drug options, so testing a ghrelin-like drug for it was reasonable. The FDA notes that one drug, alvimopan, is approved for it in the US.<Cite id="beck-2014-ipamorelin-ileus,fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" /> The development stopped, the largest trial was never published, and the compound turned up in wellness clinics and on websites for uses nobody had tested in people.<Cite id="ctgov-nct01280344,fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />

</InDetail>

</Section>

As of 22 October 2026, these are specifically unknown:

- **Whether it helps with anything people use it for.** No study in people has tested muscle, fat loss, sleep, recovery or ageing. The FDA found no effectiveness data for growth hormone deficiency either.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />
- **How the body handles it under the skin, through the nose or by mouth.** Apart from one volunteer in a doping-test study, human data exist only for drips into a vein.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing,gobburu-1999-ipamorelin-pkpd,semenistaya-2015-ghrp-nasal" />
- **Long-term safety.** The FDA found no repeat-dose toxicity, genotoxicity, carcinogenicity or reproductive studies.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />
- **Whether it is selective in people.** In the published studies, cortisol, ACTH and prolactin were measured only in pigs.<Cite id="raun-1998-ipamorelin,gobburu-1999-ipamorelin-pkpd" />
- **What months of use do to IGF-1, blood sugar and blood pressure** in people. No study has looked beyond one week in hospital.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing,beck-2014-ipamorelin-ileus" />
- **Whether the effect fades.** In rats the growth hormone response was slightly smaller after 15 days; the FDA raises the same concern for people.<Cite id="johansen-1999-ipamorelin-bone,fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />
- **Immune reactions.** No study has tested whether it triggers antibodies.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />
- **Combinations** with CJC-1295, sermorelin or other growth hormone boosters. They are sold together, but we found no study that tested them together in people.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />
- **Pregnancy and fertility.** No data; in mice, both switching on and blocking the ghrelin receptor harmed fertilisation and embryo development.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />
- **Reward and dependence.** The ghrelin receptor sits in brain reward areas; whether ipamorelin affects them is untested.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing,muller-2015-ghrelin-review" />
- **The results of the 320-patient trial**, finished in 2014.<Cite id="ctgov-nct01280344" />
- **What products contain.** Seized powders held a modified ipamorelin with an extra amino acid, and we found no survey of the content, purity or sterility of products on the market.<Cite id="gajda-2019-glycine-ghs,krug-2018-black-market-gh" />

### European Union and member states

No ipamorelin product has an EU marketing authorisation. ### United Kingdom

Selling or supplying a medicinal product without a UK authorisation is prohibited (Human Medicines Regulations 2012, regulation 46).<Cite id="uk-hmr-2012-reg-46" /> We found no UK licence for ipamorelin; the MHRA's product database could not be searched automatically, so this rests on the absence of any approval in ChEMBL.<Cite id="chembl-ipamorelin" />

<InDetail title="The United States: the 2024 advisory vote, point by point">

Ipamorelin is not FDA-approved, has no US Pharmacopeia standard and is part of no approved drug. A pharmacy may therefore compound it from raw powder only after it is placed on the 503A bulks list through rulemaking.<Cite id="usc-21-353a,fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing,fda-503a-bulks-page" />

- Two compounding businesses nominated it for that list: one the acetate, one the free base. Both withdrew their nominations on 19 September 2024, but the FDA presented both forms to its advisers anyway.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-presentations-pm" />
- FDA staff recommended against listing either form. Their reasons: the substance is not well characterised, effectiveness for growth hormone deficiency and bowel paralysis is not supported, safety signals exist, and approved treatments are available.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />
- On 29 October 2024 the Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee voted on each form separately. Free base: 0 in favour, 12 against, 1 abstention. Acetate: 0 in favour, 12 against, 1 abstention. Members cited a lack of safety and effectiveness data.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-minutes" />
- The vote is advisory and not binding.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-10-meeting" /> Here it agreed with the FDA staff. By 22 October 2026 the FDA had not proposed a rule either way.<Cite id="federalregister-ipamorelin-search-2026-10-22" />
- For outsourcing facilities, ipamorelin acetate has been in "category 2", substances that may pose significant safety risks, since 29 September 2023. The FDA cites possible immune reactions, unusual amino acids and the serious adverse events in the bowel-surgery trial.<Cite id="fda-category-2-safety-risks,fda-503b-categories-2025-03" /> It was also in category 2 for pharmacies until the nominations were withdrawn.<Cite id="fda-category-2-safety-risks" />
- Ipamorelin was not part of the July 2026 committee meeting, which considered seven other peptides.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2026-briefing-intro" />

### The July 2026 vote, for context

That later meeting is often confused with ipamorelin's own vote, so here are its facts:

- On 23 July 2026 the same committee voted 8–6, with one abstention, to recommend BPC-157 for the 503A list. It considered seven peptides and recommended six: BPC-157, KPV, TB-500, MOTS-c, Semax and epitalon. Emideltide (DSIP) was not recommended. The FDA has posted no minutes, so these results are as reported from the public meeting.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2026-07-meeting,fda-pcac-2026-questions,fda-pcac-2026-briefing-intro" />
- That vote is advisory and not binding. It went against FDA staff's own recommendation.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2026-07-meeting,fda-pcac-2026-briefing-intro" />
- Legal compounding of those peptides stays prohibited until the FDA adopts the advice and completes rulemaking.<Cite id="fda-503a-bulks-page" />
- None of them is an approved medicine in the EU, the UK or the US; Semax is registered as a medicine in Russia. A 503A listing would only allow pharmacy compounding on prescription in the US. It is not a marketing authorisation, says nothing about whether a substance works, and changes nothing in the EU or the Netherlands.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2026-semax-briefing,fr-2026-07361-pcac-notice,fda-pcac-2026-presentations-day1,usc-21-353a,nl-geneesmiddelenwet" />

None of this makes ipamorelin an approved medicine, and none of it changes anything in the EU or the Netherlands.

<Section id="risks">

<Lead question="What are the risks?">Unknown products, higher blood sugar and fluid build-up are the main worries. Long-term risks are untested.</Lead>

<IconArray title="In one trial, some problems were more common with it. The groups were small." detail="People in 100 with each problem: 56 patients on ipamorelin and 58 on placebo, given drips into a vein for up to 7 days after surgery." a="Ipamorelin" b="Placebo" source="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" level="human-controlled" rows={[

The FDA's summary gives no statistical tests for these differences, and surgery itself causes many of these problems. Overall, slightly fewer patients had any problem at all on ipamorelin (87.5%) than on placebo (94.8%).<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing,beck-2014-ipamorelin-ileus" />

<InDetail title="What has been reported in people">

Unless stated otherwise, this section draws on the FDA's 2024 review and the studies named.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />

In the bowel-surgery trial, almost everyone had some health problem (an adverse event) after their operation: 87.5% with ipamorelin and 94.8% with placebo. Most were mild to moderate and linked to the surgery or the illness. Nausea, vomiting and a swollen belly were the commonest, and slightly less frequent with ipamorelin.<Cite id="beck-2014-ipamorelin-ileus,fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" /> High blood sugar when leaving hospital (14.3% versus 8.6%), low potassium (12.5% versus 3.4%) and trouble sleeping (10.7% versus 5.2%) were more common with ipamorelin. Three patients in each group stopped treatment because of side effects. The authors reported that ipamorelin was well tolerated.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing,beck-2014-ipamorelin-ileus" />

Serious events, such as infections and leaks where the bowel was joined, occurred in 17.9% with ipamorelin and 15.5% with placebo; most happened after treatment had ended. Two patients on ipamorelin died. Both had colon cancer surgery followed by a leak at the join, and both died of complications such as sepsis and kidney failure. The FDA says it is unclear whether the deaths were related to ipamorelin. Their occurrence, with the higher rates of low potassium and high blood sugar, is one reason it calls the substance a safety concern.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />

The FDA's adverse-event database held two non-serious reports up to 30 September 2023, both with compounded products: tearing and headache after a nasal spray, and elbow pain after injections that mixed ipamorelin with sermorelin.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" /> Neither proves a cause. The FDA does not receive all reports, especially for compounded products, so two reports say little about how often problems occur.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Blood sugar, fluid and the heart: lessons from related drugs">

In published studies, ipamorelin itself has never been given for months. Related drugs have, and they show what pushing this system for months can do. In a 2-year trial, 65 healthy older adults took the ghrelin-receptor tablet MK-677 (ibutamoren) or placebo. <Claim level="human-controlled">MK-677 raised fasting blood sugar by about 0.3 mmol/L and lowered insulin sensitivity; cortisol rose; the commonest side effects were more appetite, mild swelling of the lower legs and muscle pain</Claim>.<Cite id="nass-2008-mk677-older-adults" /> In 123 older people recovering from a broken hip, <Claim level="human-controlled">a trial of MK-677 was stopped early because of a safety signal of heart failure in a few patients</Claim>.<Cite id="adunsky-2011-mk677-hip-fracture" />

For approved growth hormone medicines, the labels warn about high blood sugar and diabetes, fluid retention, raised pressure in the skull, an inflamed pancreas and, in children, hip and spine problems. The FDA sees no data showing that ipamorelin avoids these risks. It also notes a heart-rhythm effect (QT prolongation) with macimorelin, an approved drug acting on the same system.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" /> These are class warnings, not findings for ipamorelin, but the mechanism that would cause them is the one ipamorelin is used for.

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Why an effect on cancer is theorised">

The reasoning is a chain, and only its first links are established:

1. **Ipamorelin releases growth hormone in people.**<Cite id="gobburu-1999-ipamorelin-pkpd" />
2. **Growth hormone raises IGF-1, a growth signal for many cells.** With MK-677, IGF-1 rose to young-adult levels in older adults.<Cite id="medlineplus-igf1,nass-2008-mk677-older-adults" />
3. **People with higher IGF-1 have modestly more of some cancers.** <Claim level="human-limited">In a meta-analysis of 21 observational studies, higher IGF-1 was linked with more prostate cancer and breast cancer before menopause</Claim>; such studies cannot prove cause.<Cite id="renehan-2004-igf1-cancer" />
4. **Labels of approved growth hormone medicines warn of a higher risk of tumours.**<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />

<Claim level="hypothesis">So the worry is not that ipamorelin starts a cancer. It is that a growth signal could help an existing, perhaps undiagnosed, tumour grow.</Claim> No study has tested this for ipamorelin, and the FDA found no carcinogenicity studies.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Product quality and immune reactions">

The most immediate risk may be the product itself. The certificates of analysis the FDA reviewed reported purity only. They did not test impurities, clumps of peptide or bacterial toxins (endotoxins), which the FDA calls critical for anything injected.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" /> The nominators' certificates named one form in the title and gave the formula of the other.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" /> Forensic and anti-doping laboratories in Denmark, Germany and Norway found products containing "Gly-ipamorelin", ipamorelin with an extra amino acid. Its effects and safety have never been studied.<Cite id="gajda-2019-glycine-ghs,krug-2018-black-market-gh" />

Peptides can clump together during storage, and synthesis leaves near-copies of the chain. Both can trigger the immune system. The FDA is concerned about immune reactions to injected ipamorelin, more so under the skin than into a vein. Reactions range from antibodies without symptoms to life-threatening reactions, and no study has tested this.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" /> See [product risk](/en/safety/product-risk/).

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Hunger, sleep, reward and pregnancy">

- **Hunger and weight.** Ghrelin made volunteers eat 28% more, and ipamorelin raised food intake and body fat in mice.<Cite id="wren-2001-ghrelin-appetite,lall-2001-ghs-adiposity" />
- **Sleep.** Trouble sleeping was more common with ipamorelin in the bowel-surgery trial (10.7% versus 5.2%).<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />
- **Reward.** The ghrelin receptor sits in brain areas that process reward, and ghrelin increased heroin-seeking in rats. The FDA says ipamorelin could, in theory, have reinforcing effects; this is untested.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />
- **Pregnancy.** There are no reproductive studies of ipamorelin. In mice, ghrelin and a ghrelin blocker both harmed fertilisation and embryo development.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />

</InDetail>

</Section>

<Section id="harm-reduction">

<Lead question="How can someone lower the risk?">Check the product, ask a doctor for a few blood tests, and know when to stop.</Lead>

This section is for people who have decided to use ipamorelin, or already do. It is not a recommendation, and it gives no amounts, schedules or sources.

<ProductCheck extra="Check which form the label names. Ipamorelin is sold as a free base or an acetate salt. The FDA treats them as different substances." extraSources="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />

### How a doctor can help

A doctor can check what ipamorelin may change: blood sugar, fluid and blood pressure, and hormone levels.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing,nass-2008-mk677-older-adults" /> If the reason for using it is tiredness, poor sleep or muscle loss, a doctor can also look for treatable causes. True growth hormone deficiency, for example, is diagnosed with special tests and has approved treatments.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" /> See [talking to your doctor](/en/talking-to-your-doctor/).

<Callout type="stop" title="Stop and get help if" id="stop-now">
- the injection site becomes hot, red, swollen or painful, or you get a fever or chills (infection or contamination);
- you get hives, swelling of the face, lips or throat, or difficulty breathing (an allergic reaction);
- your legs or ankles swell and you become short of breath, or you get chest pain (fluid build-up or strain on the heart);
- you are very thirsty and pass a lot of urine, and you also feel sick, drowsy or confused, or breathe fast (very high blood sugar);
- your heart races or beats unevenly and it does not settle, or it comes with chest pain, breathlessness or fainting;
- you faint.

If it is severe, call your local emergency number.
</Callout>

<InDetail title="Checks that make sense, and why">

A doctor can help you choose which of these fit you:

<Checklist>

- **Blood sugar (fasting glucose or HbA1c), before and during use**, because ghrelin-receptor drugs and growth hormone can raise blood sugar. HbA1c shows the average over two to three months.<Cite id="nass-2008-mk677-older-adults,fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing,medlineplus-hba1c" />
- **Blood pressure, weight and your ankles**, because fluid retention was a common side effect of a related drug.<Cite id="nass-2008-mk677-older-adults" />
- **IGF-1**, because its level is steadier than growth hormone, which comes in pulses, so it shows the growth hormone effect better. It is a safety check, not a target.<Cite id="medlineplus-igf1" />
- **Potassium, if you feel weak or have cramps or palpitations**, because low potassium was more common with ipamorelin in the trial.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />
- **Cancer screening that is due for your age, and any unexplained lump checked first**, because of the theoretical effect of more growth hormone and IGF-1.<Cite id="renehan-2004-igf1-cancer,fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />

</Checklist>

Tell any doctor who treats you that you use it, so a change in blood sugar or a reaction is not mistaken for something else.

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Who should not use it at all, and interactions">

### Who should not use it at all

- **Anyone pregnant, breastfeeding or trying to conceive**: there are no reproductive studies, and in mice ghrelin affected fertilisation and embryo development.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />
- **Anyone with cancer, a history of cancer or an unexplained lump**, because of the growth hormone and IGF-1 mechanism above.<Cite id="renehan-2004-igf1-cancer,fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />
- **People with diabetes or prediabetes**, because blood sugar may rise.<Cite id="nass-2008-mk677-older-adults,fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />
- **People with heart failure, or kidney or liver disease that causes swelling**, because related drugs caused fluid retention and a heart-failure signal.<Cite id="nass-2008-mk677-older-adults,adunsky-2011-mk677-hip-fracture" />
- **Children and teenagers**, whose bones are still growing: growth hormone labels warn of hip and spine problems in children, and there are no data.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />
- **Athletes subject to anti-doping rules**, because it is prohibited at all times.<Cite id="wada-2026-list-en" />

### Plausible interactions (none has been studied)

- **Diabetes medicines, including insulin**, because a rise in blood sugar could work against them.<Cite id="nass-2008-mk677-older-adults,fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />
- **Other growth hormone boosters** (CJC-1295, sermorelin, tesamorelin, growth hormone itself or MK-677), which push the same system. Mixtures are sold, but we found no study of their combined effect in people.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />
- **Medicines that affect heart rhythm**, because an approved drug acting on the same system can prolong the QT interval.<Cite id="fda-pcac-2024-ipamorelin-briefing" />

</InDetail>
