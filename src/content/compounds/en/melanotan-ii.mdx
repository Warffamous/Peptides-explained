<Section id="what-it-is">

<Lead question="What is melanotan II?">A lab-made copy of a hormone that tans the skin. No country has approved it.</Lead>

Melanotan II was designed at the University of Arizona in the late 1980s, and first tested in people as a way to tan without sun.<Cite id="al-obeidi-1989-cyclic-lactam,dorr-1996-mt2-phase-1" /> It is sold online as powder in vials, in pens and as nasal sprays.<Cite id="mhra-2024-foi-melanotan-ii,deville-2024-barbie-drug-identification,yassin-alsabbagh-2025-oral-melanoma" /> In online forums, people describe using it for a tan before sun holidays or fitness competitions, and sunbed use comes up often.<Cite id="gilhooley-2021-forum-study" />

<InDetail title="The molecule, and how it differs from its two relatives">

Melanotan II is a chain of seven amino acids: <Seq>Ac-Nle-cyclo[Asp-His-D-Phe-Arg-Trp-Lys]-NH2</Seq>. Its formula is C50H69N15O9 and its mass <Q>1024.2 Da</Q>.<Cite id="pubchem-melanotan-ii" /> It copies positions 4 to 10 of alpha-MSH, the hormone that darkens skin, with three changes:<Cite id="dorr-1996-mt2-phase-1,al-obeidi-1989-cyclic-lactam" />

- **A ring.** The side chains of aspartic acid and lysine are joined by a lactam bond, so six of the seven amino acids form a closed ring. This ring held the molecule in a shape that made it about 90 times as potent as alpha-MSH in a lizard-skin test, with a longer-lasting effect.<Cite id="al-obeidi-1989-cyclic-lactam" />
- **A mirror-image building block.** Phenylalanine is in its D-form. The developers describe melanotan I and II as resistant to the enzymes that break down the natural hormone.<Cite id="pubchem-melanotan-ii,hadley-2006-melanocortin-therapeutics,haskell-luevano-1996-mc1r-dissociation" />
- **Norleucine at the front**, capped with an acetyl group, and an amide at the end.<Cite id="pubchem-melanotan-ii" />

**Afamelanotide**, once called melanotan I, is the full 13-amino-acid hormone with two changes and no ring.<Cite id="haskell-luevano-1996-mc1r-dissociation,hadley-2006-melanocortin-therapeutics" /> It is an implant for erythropoietic protoporphyria (EPP), a rare disease in which light causes burning pain, approved in the US, the UK, Canada and Australia.<Cite id="fda-drugsatfda-scenesse,uk-mhra-substance-index-status-c-2026-10-23,ca-hc-pdl-2026-08-27-additions,au-artg-search-status-c-2026-10-23" />
{/* residue-ok: EMA evidence */}
The European medicines regulator's assessment describes how it works: it increases dark melanin in the skin, which blocks light.<Cite id="ema-epar-scenesse" />

**Bremelanotide** (PT-141) has exactly the same ring as melanotan II, but ends in a free acid instead of an amide.<Cite id="king-2007-melanocortin-erection,pubchem-melanotan-ii" /> See [PT-141](/en/compounds/pt-141/). Its US label is a rough guide to what a molecule this close may do in people, because it was tested in large trials; melanotan II never was.<Cite id="dailymed-vyleesi-label" />

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Where it came from">

In 1989 chemists at the University of Arizona reported a series of ring-shaped alpha-MSH fragments built for potency and long action.<Cite id="al-obeidi-1989-cyclic-lactam" /> The developers later wrote that melanotan I might give a "therapeutic tan" that could lower the risk of skin cancer.<Cite id="hadley-2006-melanocortin-therapeutics" /> In the first test of melanotan II in people, in 1996, two of three men tanned, and the men also had spontaneous erections.<Cite id="dorr-1996-mt2-phase-1" /> One of the developers later wrote that the sexual effects "were discovered accidentally" during the tanning work.<Cite id="hadley-2005-discovery" />

After two small erection trials (1998 and 2000), development moved on to its free-acid form, PT-141, which became the US medicine Vyleesi in 2019, while tanning work continued with melanotan I, now afamelanotide.<Cite id="wessells-2000-mt2-organic-ed,hadley-2006-melanocortin-therapeutics,fda-drugsatfda-vyleesi,fda-drugsatfda-scenesse" /> No further trial of melanotan II itself has been published. Products under its name kept being sold: in 2024 the UK medicines regulator wrote that it had been removing melanotan products from the market for over ten years.<Cite id="mhra-2024-foi-melanotan-ii" />

</InDetail>

<Section id="mechanism">

<Lead question="How does it work?">It switches on several receptors. In skin it adds dark pigment; in the brain it causes erections.</Lead>

<Diagram id="melanotan-ii-receptor-map" caption="One drug, four receptors. Each one leads to a different effect.">

</Diagram>

<Cite id="hruby-1995-melanocortin-antagonists,wolf-horrell-2016-mc1r,abdel-malek-1995-melanocyte-mitogenic,dorr-1996-mt2-phase-1,fan-1997-melanocortin-feeding,wessells-2003-mt2-spinal-erection,wessells-2000-mt2-review-20-men,greenfield-2009-mc4r-blood-pressure" />

The body's own hormone, alpha-MSH, works on several melanocortin receptors. Melanotan II is a strong copy that switches on four of them.<Cite id="hruby-1995-melanocortin-antagonists" /> So the effects come as a package: in the first trial, the same men who tanned also felt sick and had spontaneous erections.<Cite id="dorr-1996-mt2-phase-1" />

<InDetail title="Step by step: the chain and what each step rests on">

<Mechanism />

### Receptors: why one molecule does many things

<Claim level="in-vitro">In cells carrying cloned human or mouse receptors, the MC1, MC3, MC4 and MC5 receptors all responded to melanotan II</Claim>; MC2, the receptor for the stress hormone ACTH, responds mainly to ACTH.<Cite id="hruby-1995-melanocortin-antagonists" /> <Claim level="in-vitro">On the human MC1 receptor it let go very slowly: six hours after washing, 86% was still bound, against 25% of alpha-MSH</Claim>.<Cite id="haskell-luevano-1996-mc1r-dissociation" /> The authors suggest that this slow release helps explain its long-lasting effects. The bremelanotide label states the same pattern for that close relative: it "nonselectively activates several receptor subtypes", most potently MC1R and then MC4R.<Cite id="dailymed-vyleesi-label" />

### Pigment cells: darker, and more of them

MC1R is a G-protein-coupled receptor. Switched on, it raises cAMP inside the melanocyte, and the cell makes more melanin and passes it to the surrounding skin cells.<Cite id="wolf-horrell-2016-mc1r" /> <Claim level="in-vitro">In cultured human melanocytes, alpha-MSH increased tyrosinase, the enzyme that starts melanin production, by 70–450%, and the number of cells by 50–270%, depending on the donor</Claim>.<Cite id="abdel-malek-1995-melanocyte-mitogenic" /> That second effect matters for the mole question below: the same signal that darkens pigment cells also makes them multiply, at least in a dish. In people, the only tanning data are from the 3-man pilot (see the evidence below).<Cite id="dorr-1996-mt2-phase-1" />

### Brain and spinal cord: erections, appetite and nausea

<Claim level="animal">In awake rats, melanotan II caused erections whether it was given into the brain, the spinal fluid or a vein; a receptor blocker in the spinal fluid stopped the spinal effect, and injecting it into the penis of anaesthetised rats did nothing</Claim>. In rats, then, the erection signal starts in the brain and the lower spinal cord, not in the penis.<Cite id="wessells-2003-mt2-spinal-erection" />

<Claim level="animal">In mice, melanotan II placed in the brain reduced eating in four different models of overeating, and a blocker made mice eat more</Claim>.<Cite id="fan-1997-melanocortin-feeding" /> <Claim level="animal">Given into a vein in rats, it still reduced eating, although almost none entered the brain: it reached small areas where the blood-brain barrier is open</Claim>.<Cite id="trivedi-2003-mt2-anorectic-site" /> <Claim level="animal">Rats also learned to avoid a flavour paired with melanotan II, a sign that it makes them feel ill</Claim>, which fits the nausea seen in men.<Cite id="benoit-2003-mt2-taste-aversion,wessells-2000-mt2-organic-ed" />

### Blood pressure: shown for relatives, not measured for melanotan II

<Claim level="human-controlled">In 28 overweight or obese volunteers, a different MC4 receptor agonist given daily for seven days raised blood pressure by about 9 mm Hg systolic and 7 mm Hg diastolic compared with placebo, at the highest dose they tolerated</Claim>. In the same study, people born with a faulty MC4 receptor had high blood pressure less often than equally overweight people without the fault.<Cite id="greenfield-2009-mc4r-blood-pressure" /> Bremelanotide raises blood pressure after each dose, by up to 6 mm Hg systolic, peaking 2–4 hours after the dose.<Cite id="dailymed-vyleesi-label" /> <Claim level="hypothesis">Melanotan II probably does the same through the same receptor</Claim>, but no trial measured it.

</InDetail>

<Lead question="Does it work?">It darkened skin and caused erections in small trials of 23 men in all.</Lead>

The effects were clear in these small trials, but the evidence is thin. A tan and erections both fit the biology, and a close relative, bremelanotide, went on to large trials and US approval.<Cite id="dailymed-vyleesi-label" /> But nobody has tested whether a melanotan II tan protects the skin, how long it lasts, or what months of use do.

<InDetail title="Lab and animal studies">

Melanotan II is a common research tool for the brain's melanocortin system. A PubMed search on 22 October 2026 found 189 non-review records with "melanotan II" in the title or abstract, and 364 when the abbreviation MTII is counted in melanocortin papers; almost all mention animals or cells.<Cite id="pubmed-melanotan-ii-count-2026-10-22" /> The developing group at the University of Arizona wrote 10 of the 189 and 45 of the 364.<Cite id="pubmed-melanotan-ii-count-2026-10-22" />

That is why the laboratory picture is well replicated. <Claim level="animal">The appetite effect seen in mice in Oregon was repeated in rats by a Merck team and, with a taste-aversion test, by a Cincinnati team</Claim>.<Cite id="fan-1997-melanocortin-feeding,trivedi-2003-mt2-anorectic-site,benoit-2003-mt2-taste-aversion" /> The rat erection work and the receptor studies come from the developing group and its collaborators.<Cite id="wessells-2003-mt2-spinal-erection,hruby-1995-melanocortin-antagonists,haskell-luevano-1996-mc1r-dissociation" />

What the animal work does not cover matters more for users: a PubMed search for toxicity, cancer and pregnancy studies of melanotan II on 22 October 2026 found none.<Cite id="pubmed-melanotan-ii-count-2026-10-22" /> <Claim level="animal">One study in 12 mice with an implanted melanoma found the opposite of a cancer-promoting effect: melanotan II put on the skin slowed the tumour</Claim>. It tested an existing tumour in mice, not normal moles in people.<Cite id="wu-2020-topical-mtii-melanoma" />

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="The three trials in people">

All three trials come from the University of Arizona group that designed the molecule, and the study table above gives each one's findings and limits.<Cite id="dorr-1996-mt2-phase-1,wessells-1998-mt2-psychogenic-ed,wessells-2000-mt2-organic-ed" />

- **1996, 3 healthy men, two weeks.** <Claim level="human-limited">Two tanned; mild nausea was common; yawning and stretching came with spontaneous erections</Claim>.<Cite id="dorr-1996-mt2-phase-1" />
- **1998, 10 men with erection problems of no known physical cause.** <Claim level="human-controlled">Eight had erections with melanotan II; rigid erections lasted a mean 38.0 minutes, against 3.0 with placebo</Claim>.<Cite id="wessells-1998-mt2-psychogenic-ed" />
- **2000, 10 men with physical risk factors.** <Claim level="human-controlled">Erections followed 12 of 19 melanotan II injections and 1 of 21 placebo injections, and desire rose; 4 of 19 injections caused severe nausea</Claim>.<Cite id="wessells-2000-mt2-organic-ed" />

What these trials cannot tell you: anything about women (none took part), repeated use beyond two weeks, how well a tan protects, or any rare harm. Twenty-three people can only reveal common, short-term effects.

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Where the evidence is promising, and what the gap costs">

The biology behind melanotan II has produced real medicines. Afamelanotide, its straight-chain relative, is approved for EPP in the US, the UK, Canada and Australia.<Cite id="fda-drugsatfda-scenesse,uk-mhra-substance-index-status-c-2026-10-23,ca-hc-pdl-2026-08-27-additions,au-artg-search-status-c-2026-10-23" />
{/* residue-ok: EMA evidence */}
The European medicines regulator's assessment explains why it helps: the extra melanin it causes lets patients tolerate more light.<Cite id="ema-epar-scenesse" /> <Claim level="human-controlled">In a randomised trial completed by 65 people, afamelanotide raised skin melanin, and in volunteers who burn easily it more than halved the sunburn cells caused by a standard test dose of UV</Claim>.<Cite id="barnetson-2006-afamelanotide-photoprotection" /> Bremelanotide is a US medicine.<Cite id="dailymed-vyleesi-label" /> Setmelanotide (Imcivree), an MC4 receptor agonist, has been approved in the US since 25 November 2020 to reduce excess weight in some rare genetic conditions. Since March 2026 it is also approved for obesity caused by injury to, or a disorder of, the hypothalamus, a part of the brain.<Cite id="fda-drugsatfda-imcivree,fda-label-imcivree-2026-03" />

So the idea of a melanocortin tan is not fantasy, and a tested product for people who burn easily could have value. The cost of the gap is that melanotan II itself stopped at 23 men in 2000, while it kept being sold. Its users now get a strong, unselective version of that biology, from products of uncertain strength and purity, without the trials that would show what it does over years.

</InDetail>

### United Kingdom

Most of what is known about harm comes from case reports of people using unregulated products, often together with sunbeds or other drugs.<Cite id="hjuler-2014-melanotan-melanoma,vadner-2026-five-melanomas,nelson-2012-melanotan-rhabdomyolysis" /> The FDA lists the same serious events.<Cite id="fda-category-2-safety-risks" /> Neither can say how often they happen.

<InDetail title="Side effects in the trials">

In the trials, the common effects were nausea, stretching and yawning, less appetite and spontaneous erections; the investigators' pooled account puts severe nausea at 12.9% at the dose they studied.<Cite id="dorr-1996-mt2-phase-1,wessells-1998-mt2-psychogenic-ed,wessells-2000-mt2-review-20-men" /> In rats, pure melanotan II produces a learned dislike of a flavour paired with it, whichever way it is given: a sign that feeling sick is part of the drug's own action, not a quirk of one product.<Cite id="benoit-2003-mt2-taste-aversion" />

For comparison, its close relative bremelanotide caused nausea in 40% of women in the large trials behind its US approval, and dark patches on the face, gums or breasts in 1% at occasional use but 38% after eight days of daily use; the patches did not always fade after stopping.<Cite id="dailymed-vyleesi-label" />

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Moles and melanoma: why the worry makes sense, and why it is unproven">

The worry follows a chain that is partly established and partly a hypothesis:

<Diagram id="melanotan-ii-mole-question" caption="Why melanotan II raises a skin cancer question. The last step has not been tested.">

1. **Melanotan II switches on MC1R on every pigment cell, including those in moles** (shown in cells).
2. **The cells make more melanin, so moles darken** (seen in people: case reports).
3. **The same signal makes pigment cells multiply, at least in a dish; new moles appear** (cells; case reports of eruptive moles).
4. **Darker and new moles are harder to judge, so a melanoma may be found later** (shown in one dermoscopy case).
5. **Whether this raises the chance of melanoma itself is not known** (hypothesis; sunbeds and many moles confuse the picture).

</Diagram>

<Cite id="wolf-horrell-2016-mc1r,abdel-malek-1995-melanocyte-mitogenic,burian-2019-eruptive-naevi-review,mang-2012-dermoscopy-changes,habbema-2017-melanotan-review" />

**What is seen.** <Claim level="human-limited">Many case reports describe existing moles darkening and new moles appearing during melanotan use</Claim>, in one report within 24 hours of a single injection.<Cite id="cousen-2009-eruptive-naevi,schulze-2014-naevi-24-hours,burian-2019-eruptive-naevi-review" /> A review of all published eruptive-mole cases found 179 people; immunosuppressant drugs, chemotherapy or melanotan were the suspected trigger in 41%, 16% had at least one abnormal (dysplastic) mole, and five had a melanoma.<Cite id="burian-2019-eruptive-naevi-review" /> In one case followed with a dermatoscope, the changes made it difficult to tell a mole from a melanoma.<Cite id="mang-2012-dermoscopy-changes" /> A related approved medicine shows a similar effect: setmelanotide acts mainly on MC4 and much more weakly on MC1, yet its US label warns that it darkens existing moles and can cause new ones, and asks for a full skin check before and during treatment.<Cite id="fda-label-imcivree-2026-03" /> Pigment can also appear on the gums and inner cheeks, and in one man the gum colour was still there three months after stopping; he also smoked, which can darken the gums too.<Cite id="bonchev-2026-oral-pigmentation" />

**Melanoma reports.** <Claim level="human-limited">Melanoma has been reported during or shortly after melanotan use in several case reports</Claim>: a 2017 review counted four.<Cite id="habbema-2017-melanotan-review" /> Reported cases include a melanoma in a woman (2011), a melanoma in situ in a young woman (2012), a 20-year-old woman in Denmark who also used sunbeds, a woman whose mouth melanoma followed a melanotan II nasal spray, and in 2026 a 49-year-old man with five early melanomas (melanoma in situ), whose moles had "turned black" within about a week of starting.<Cite id="paurobally-2011-melanotan-melanoma,ong-2012-melanoma-in-situ,hjuler-2014-melanotan-melanoma,yassin-alsabbagh-2025-oral-melanoma,vadner-2026-five-melanomas" /> A man with a past melanoma had new atypical moles after using a synthetic alpha-MSH peptide, and his moles lightened after he stopped; the authors concluded that such peptides "can drive proliferation" of abnormal pigment cells in predisposed people.<Cite id="cardones-2009-eruptive-nevi" />

**Why that is not proof.** Melanoma is common, and the people in these reports often had other strong risks. In 27 observational studies, people who had ever used sunbeds had about a fifth more melanoma, and nearly twice as much when they started before age 35.<Cite id="boniol-2012-sunbed-melanoma" /> The 2026 case also had many moles, fair skin, a grandmother with melanoma and unregulated testosterone use. His doctors add that the melanomas may already have been there, and only became visible when the moles darkened.<Cite id="vadner-2026-five-melanomas" /> The authors of the 2017 review conclude that conclusive evidence linking melanotan to melanoma is lacking.<Cite id="habbema-2017-melanotan-review" />

**The argument the other way.** MC1R signalling also helps pigment cells repair UV damage to their DNA: <Claim level="in-vitro">in cultured human melanocytes, alpha-MSH reduced UV-induced DNA damage and cell death</Claim>, and people whose MC1R works poorly are more prone to melanoma.<Cite id="kadekaro-2005-msh-dna-repair,wolf-horrell-2016-mc1r" /> <Claim level="animal">In 12 mice with an implanted melanoma, melanotan II put on the skin slowed the tumour</Claim>; in a dish, blocking MC1R removed its effect on melanoma cells.<Cite id="wu-2020-topical-mtii-melanoma" /> So stimulating MC1R could in theory protect as well as harm. Which effect wins for melanotan II in real use, often with sunbeds, has never been studied.

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Why a melanotan tan cannot be counted as sun protection">

A tan from melanotan II looks like a sun tan, but nobody has measured what it protects against. <Claim level="human-limited">On light skin, even a natural tan offers only modest protection</Claim>.<Cite id="young-2020-pigmentation-sunscreens" /> <Claim level="human-limited">In a study of 12 volunteers, tans built with UVA light gave less protection than an SPF of 1.5, and tans built with UVB gave only very modest protection</Claim>; the authors called a pre-holiday tan from UVA-rich sunlamps counterproductive.<Cite id="coelho-2015-uva-tan" /> How a tan is made changes what it does.

With afamelanotide, a related molecule tested in a randomised trial, the extra melanin did reduce sunburn cells in volunteers who burn easily.<Cite id="barnetson-2006-afamelanotide-photoprotection" /> That has never been tested for melanotan II. In online forums, users often mention sunbeds as well, and sunbeds add UV damage of their own.<Cite id="gilhooley-2021-forum-study,boniol-2012-sunbed-melanoma" />

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Erections that do not stop (priapism)">

In rats, melanotan II starts erections from the brain and spinal cord, not from the penis. In men, it caused erections without any sexual stimulation.<Cite id="wessells-2003-mt2-spinal-erection,wessells-2000-mt2-review-20-men" /> In the 1996 pilot, spontaneous erections came and went over 1–5 hours after an injection.<Cite id="dorr-1996-mt2-phase-1" /> But <Claim level="human-limited">several case reports describe priapism, an erection that does not go down, after melanotan injections</Claim>.<Cite id="dreyer-2019-melanotan-priapism,devlin-2013-melanotan-priapism,mallory-2021-melanotan-priapism" />

In this kind of priapism ("low-flow" or ischaemic), blood is trapped in the penis and its tissue runs short of oxygen, which can lead to scarring. In a Glasgow case, drainage and medicine worked, but erections had not recovered four weeks later.<Cite id="dreyer-2019-melanotan-priapism,mallory-2021-melanotan-priapism" /> In a Miami case, a 55-year-old man arrived after 30 hours; medicine failed, he needed surgery, and 15 weeks later he had new erection problems with scarring.<Cite id="mallory-2021-melanotan-priapism" /> An erection that lasts more than 3 hours needs emergency care, because delay can cause lasting damage.<Cite id="nhs-priapism" />

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Heart, blood pressure, brain and kidneys">

<Claim level="human-limited">A Chicago man who injected an unusually large amount of melanotan II developed a racing heart, sweating, tremor and anxiety within two hours; his muscle broke down (rhabdomyolysis), his kidney function fell, and he spent three days in intensive care</Claim>. Testing confirmed the product was melanotan II; his urine also tested positive for opiates.<Cite id="nelson-2012-melanotan-rhabdomyolysis" /> Another hospital treated a 27-year-old man for similar "sympathomimetic" symptoms two hours after an injection.<Cite id="eijmael-2022-barbie-drug" />

PRES, a swelling at the back of the brain, has been reported after melanotan injections.<Cite id="kaski-2013-melanotan-pres" /> PRES is linked to swings in blood pressure, among other causes. Mild forms may cause only a headache or a fit; most cases get better, but severe forms can cause bleeding in the brain.<Cite id="fugate-2015-pres" /> <Claim level="hypothesis">A rise in blood pressure through MC4 receptors would be a plausible route</Claim>, because related molecules raise blood pressure in people; this has not been tested.<Cite id="greenfield-2009-mc4r-blood-pressure,dailymed-vyleesi-label" />

A man in Sweden had a kidney infarction, part of a kidney dying from a blocked blood supply, which his doctors judged most likely caused by melanotan II.<Cite id="peters-2020-melanotan-renal-infarction" /> Australia's medicines regulator lists kidney problems and swelling of the brain among the harms reported with melanotan II.<Cite id="au-tga-2025-01-melanotan-blog" />

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="What is in the products">

Vials bought from three online shops did contain melanotan II, but only 43–88% of the amount on the label, and vials from two shops held 4.1–5.9% unknown impurities.<Cite id="breindahl-2015-melanotan-products" /> A forensic laboratory found vials labelled melanotan II that were 30% pure.<Cite id="deville-2024-barbie-drug-identification" /> Australia's medicines regulator tested five seized nasal sprays with the same label: the amount of melanotan II in them varied more than twofold.<Cite id="au-tga-2026-08-melanotan-ii-safety-alert" />
{/* residue-ok: European regulator evidence (what a tested product contained) */}
A European medicines regulator found insulin in products sold as melanotan; insulin can cause a coma.<Cite id="fagg-melanotan-warning" /> Samples seized by Italian police included both melanotan II and bremelanotide.<Cite id="mestria-2021-seized-melanotan-bremelanotide" />

Peptides can clump together, and making them leaves near-copies of the chain as impurities. For these reasons the FDA says compounded melanotan II "may pose risk for immunogenicity", an unwanted immune reaction, for some routes of use.<Cite id="fda-category-2-safety-risks" /> Injecting anything also brings the risk of infection; see [product risk](/en/safety/product-risk/) and [sterile technique](/en/safety/sterile-technique/).

</InDetail>

<Section id="harm-reduction">

<Lead question="How can someone lower the risk?">Check the product, have your skin checked first, and know the emergency signs.</Lead>

This section gives no amounts, schedules or sources. It is for people who already use melanotan II or have a product.

<ProductCheck extra="Even a full label can be wrong. Tested vials held less than the label said, and sprays with the same label held very different amounts. One sample was only 30% pure. Some products with this name held insulin." extraSources="breindahl-2015-melanotan-products,au-tga-2026-08-melanotan-ii-safety-alert,deville-2024-barbie-drug-identification,fagg-melanotan-warning" />

### A doctor can help you keep watch

Your doctor or a dermatologist can check your moles and compare them over time. Tell them you use melanotan. The changes it causes can look like melanoma. A doctor who knows about it can judge them better.<Cite id="mang-2012-dermoscopy-changes,nhs-moles" /> A doctor can also check your blood pressure. See [talking to your doctor](/en/talking-to-your-doctor/).

<Callout type="stop" title="Stop and get help if" id="stop-now">
- **An erection lasts more than 3 hours, or is painful and will not go down.** This is priapism. Call your local emergency number or go to an emergency department now; delay can damage the penis for good.
- **Your heart races, you sweat, shake or feel very agitated**, especially with muscle pain or dark, cola-coloured urine. This can be a toxic reaction with muscle breakdown. Get emergency care.
- **You get a sudden, severe headache, a fit, confusion or problems with your sight.** These can be signs of brain swelling or very high blood pressure. Call your local emergency number.
- **A mole is new, or changes size, shape or colour, itches or bleeds**, or a dark patch appears in your mouth. Stop melanotan II and see a doctor soon.
</Callout>

<WarningSigns only="general,injection,allergic,cardiovascular,metabolic,neurological" />

<InDetail title="Skin checks that make sense, and why">

<Checklist>

- **A full skin check before you start**, by your doctor or a dermatologist, with photos of your moles. New and darker moles can appear within days. Photos give you something to compare with.<Cite id="schulze-2014-naevi-24-hours,mang-2012-dermoscopy-changes" />
- **Regular checks while you use it and afterwards.** If you have many moles, ask for dermoscopy (a lit magnifier). The changes can make a mole hard to tell from melanoma.<Cite id="mang-2012-dermoscopy-changes" />
- **Look in your mouth too.** Brown patches on the gums and cheeks have been reported, and one melanoma inside the mouth.<Cite id="bonchev-2026-oral-pigmentation,yassin-alsabbagh-2025-oral-melanoma" />
- **See a doctor about any mole that changes size, shape or colour, itches, bleeds or crusts.**<Cite id="nhs-moles" />
- **Sunbeds add their own risk.** They raise melanoma risk on their own, and a melanotan tan has not been shown to protect you.<Cite id="boniol-2012-sunbed-melanoma,coelho-2015-uva-tan" />

</Checklist>

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Other checks, and why">

- **Blood pressure, before and while using it.** Related molecules that switch on MC4 receptors raised blood pressure in people. The US label for bremelanotide says not to use it with uncontrolled high blood pressure or known heart or blood vessel disease.<Cite id="greenfield-2009-mc4r-blood-pressure,dailymed-vyleesi-label" />
- **Kidney tests and a muscle enzyme test (CK)** if you had muscle pain, dark urine or a racing heart after an injection. Muscle breakdown releases a protein that can harm the kidneys.<Cite id="nelson-2012-melanotan-rhabdomyolysis,medlineplus-rhabdomyolysis" />
- **Report side effects**, even if you are not sure the product caused them. In the US, report to [FDA MedWatch](https://www.fda.gov/safety/medwatch-fda-safety-information-and-adverse-event-reporting-program); in the UK, to the [Yellow Card scheme](https://yellowcard.mhra.gov.uk/); in Canada, to Health Canada's [Canada Vigilance Program](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/drugs-health-products/medeffect-canada/adverse-reaction-reporting.html); in Australia, to the [TGA](https://www.tga.gov.au/safety/report-problem/report-adverse-event-or-safety-problem). For Ireland and New Zealand, see [our notes page](/en/legal/ireland-and-new-zealand/).<Cite id="fda-medwatch,mhra-yellow-card,mhra-2024-foi-melanotan-ii,ca-hc-medeffect-reporting,ca-hc-canada-vigilance,au-tga-report-adverse-event" />
- **If you stop**, you can take unused products to a pharmacy for disposal. The TGA and Health Canada both advise this.<Cite id="au-tga-2026-08-melanotan-ii-safety-alert,ca-hc-recalls-database" />

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Who should not use it at all">

- **Anyone who has had melanoma, has many or atypical moles, or has melanoma in the family.** Melanotan II stimulates the pigment cells that melanoma grows from. The people in the reported cases often had these risks.<Cite id="cardones-2009-eruptive-nevi,vadner-2026-five-melanomas,wolf-horrell-2016-mc1r" />
- **Anyone with high blood pressure or heart disease.** Related molecules raise blood pressure. Bremelanotide must not be used with uncontrolled high blood pressure or known heart or blood vessel disease.<Cite id="dailymed-vyleesi-label,greenfield-2009-mc4r-blood-pressure" />
- **Anyone pregnant, trying to conceive or breastfeeding.** There are no data for melanotan II, and the US label advises stopping bremelanotide if pregnancy is suspected.<Cite id="dailymed-vyleesi-label" />
- **Children and teenagers**, for whom there are no data at all.
- **Athletes subject to anti-doping rules.** By our reading of the List, it is prohibited at all times under S0.<Cite id="wada-2026-list-en,wada-2027-list-en" />

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Possible interactions (none has been studied)">

- **Erection medicines** (such as sildenafil or tadalafil) or penile injections: melanotan II starts erections by another route, so the effects may add up and raise the risk of priapism.<Cite id="wessells-2003-mt2-spinal-erection,mallory-2021-melanotan-priapism" />
- **Stimulants and other drugs that raise heart rate or blood pressure**, because of the reported sympathomimetic reactions.<Cite id="nelson-2012-melanotan-rhabdomyolysis,eijmael-2022-barbie-drug" />
- **Tablets taken by mouth, especially naltrexone**: bremelanotide may slow stomach emptying and lowers the amount of oral naltrexone that is absorbed. Whether melanotan II does the same is unknown.<Cite id="dailymed-vyleesi-label" />
- **Mixtures with other peptides or hormones**, which make any reaction impossible to attribute; the 2026 melanoma case also involved melanotan I and testosterone.<Cite id="vadner-2026-five-melanomas" />

</InDetail>

<InDetail title="Legal routes, if you want what it promises">

None of the four countries has approved melanotan II, so no doctor can prescribe an approved product.<Cite id="fr-2016-melanotan-debarment,uk-mhra-substance-index-status-c-2026-10-23,ca-dpd-search-status-c-2026-10-23,au-tga-2026-08-melanotan-ii-safety-alert" /> For the conditions its relatives treat, there are approved medicines. Afamelanotide (Scenesse) is approved for EPP in the US, the UK, Canada and Australia.<Cite id="fda-drugsatfda-scenesse,uk-mhra-substance-index-status-c-2026-10-23,ca-hc-pdl-2026-08-27-additions,au-artg-search-status-c-2026-10-23" /> Bremelanotide (Vyleesi) is a US medicine for low sexual desire in some premenopausal women; it is not approved in the UK, Canada or Australia.<Cite id="dailymed-vyleesi-label,mhra-products-search-bremelanotide,ca-dpd-search-status-c-2026-10-23,au-artg-search-status-c-2026-10-23" /> For erection problems or low desire, a doctor can look for causes and offer approved treatment on [prescription](/en/legal-routes/#prescription).

Our page on [treatment routes](/en/legal-routes/) explains the other legal routes and their limits: [off-label use](/en/legal-routes/#off-label), [US pharmacy compounding](/en/legal-routes/#us-compounding), [UK "specials"](/en/legal-routes/#uk-specials), [Canada's Special Access Program](/en/legal-routes/#canada-special-access), [Australia's Special Access Scheme](/en/legal-routes/#australia-sas), [importing for yourself](/en/legal-routes/#personal-import) and [clinical trials](/en/legal-routes/#trials). Each has strict conditions, and none turns an unapproved product into an approved one.

</InDetail>
